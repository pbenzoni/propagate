The proposed deal would have seen Ukraine supply components for naval drones manufactured at Greek shipyards, with Greek companies providing electronic and optical systems. The arrangement was designed to produce an enhanced version of Ukraine's Magura-type attack drones. However, negotiations broke down when Ukrainian officials demanded veto power over how Greece's own military could deploy the weapons—a condition Athens firmly rejected as an unacceptable infringement on national sovereignty.
