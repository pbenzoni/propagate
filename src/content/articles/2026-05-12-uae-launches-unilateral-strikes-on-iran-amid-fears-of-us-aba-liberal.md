The strikes represent a significant shift in regional security dynamics, as the UAE appears increasingly willing to act unilaterally rather than rely on American deterrence.

Iran acknowledged the attack and responded with missile and drone strikes targeting the UAE and Kuwait, both of which host major American military installations.
