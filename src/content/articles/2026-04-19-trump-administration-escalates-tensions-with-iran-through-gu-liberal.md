The Trump administration has seized an Iranian-flagged cargo vessel in the Gulf of Oman after the ship attempted to navigate past U.S. naval forces enforcing a blockade around Iran, according to an announcement from President Trump.

The USS Spruance, a guided missile destroyer, intercepted the vessel named Touska in the Gulf of Oman. According to Trump's social media post, the Navy gave warnings before damaging the ship's engine room when the crew refused to comply. The blockade, which targets all vessels entering or leaving Iranian ports, was imposed on April 13 following the collapse of diplomatic negotiations between Washington and Tehran that had been held in Islamabad.

The maritime seizure comes amid escalating rhetoric from the administration. Trump warned on social media that there would be "No more MR. Nice Guy" if Iran didn't accept what he characterized as a "fair and reasonable deal," even as he announced a new round of talks. Iran's ambassador to Pakistan has indicated that resolution remains unlikely while the naval blockade continues.

The confrontation has wider regional implications. Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has called the blockade a violation of ceasefire terms and reimposed its own closure of the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global shipping chokepoint. Tehran had briefly reopened the strait following a U.S.-brokered truce between Israel and Lebanon, which Iran had originally insisted be part of any ceasefire agreement with Washington.
