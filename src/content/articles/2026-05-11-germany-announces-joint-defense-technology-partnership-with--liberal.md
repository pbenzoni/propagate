Germany and Ukraine have agreed to jointly develop advanced unmanned aerial systems, German Defense Minister Boris Pistorius announced during a visit to Kyiv this week. The partnership, formalized under a program called "Brave Germany," will focus on supporting defense technology startups and developing long-range capabilities.

The collaboration represents an evolution in Germany's support for Ukraine as it continues to defend itself against Russian aggression. Germany has emerged as Ukraine's second-largest military supporter after the United States, having committed approximately €20 billion ($23.5 billion) in military assistance between January 2022 and February 2025, according to the Kiel Institute for the World Economy.

Pistorius characterized the partnership as mutually beneficial, noting that Germany is also modernizing its own defense capabilities in response to security concerns in Europe. "Germany and Ukraine are strategic partners who both benefit from the cooperation," the minister stated, emphasizing the program's focus on innovation in unmanned systems technology.

The announcement comes as European nations increasingly coordinate their defense strategies in light of regional security challenges. The joint development initiative will prioritize startups demonstrating promising technological innovations in the defense sector, according to German officials.
