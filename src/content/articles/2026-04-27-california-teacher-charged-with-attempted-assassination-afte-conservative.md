Federal prosecutors have charged him with attempted assassination of the president, interstate transportation of weapons, and discharge of a firearm during a violent crime. A Secret Service agent was shot during the incident.

"This man was a floor above the ballroom, with hundreds of federal agents between him and the president of the U.S.," Blanche said at a press conference following Monday's court hearing. Evidence suggests Allen fired the shotgun, though investigators are still examining ballistics to determine exactly who shot the Secret Service agent. "It appears that there were five shots that law enforcement fired," Blanche noted.

U.S. Magistrate Judge Matthew Sharbaugh ordered Allen detained while the case proceeds. His preliminary hearing is scheduled for May 11, with another hearing Thursday to determine eligibility for pretrial release.
