A drone struck electrical infrastructure at the United Arab Emirates' Barakah Nuclear Power Plant on Sunday, according to Emirati officials, in an incident that underscores the dangerous volatility gripping the Middle East as diplomatic efforts continue to falter.

The UAE Defense Ministry reported that three drones crossed into the country's airspace, with two intercepted but a third striking an electrical generator outside the nuclear facility's inner perimeter. Authorities reported no injuries or radioactive contamination from the attack. The UAE Foreign Ministry condemned what it called an "unprovoked terrorist attack" that threatened national security, though officials stopped short of identifying who was responsible.

The incident comes amid deepening regional tensions as US-Iran peace negotiations remain stalled, with both nations accusing the other of blocking progress. According to multiple reports, the Trump administration has considered abandoning last month's fragile truce and resuming military action. Iran's foreign minister has accused Gulf states of complicity in recent attacks, stating that the UAE "was directly involved in the aggression against my country" and failed to condemn strikes launched in late February.
