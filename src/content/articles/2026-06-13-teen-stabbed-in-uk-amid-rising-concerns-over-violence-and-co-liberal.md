A 17-year-old girl was stabbed in the neck in Brierfield, northwest England, on Friday afternoon, according to Lancashire Police. A 30-year-old British-born man has been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder following the incident. Both the victim and the suspect are of Pakistani heritage, the BBC reported.

Police confirmed they responded to reports of the stabbing and that the victim received medical treatment before being discharged from the hospital. "This was a very serious incident but thankfully the girl's injuries were not life-threatening," police said in a statement. "After receiving treatment and care, she has been discharged from hospital and is recovering at home."

Oliver Ryan, the Member of Parliament representing Brierfield, urged the public not to "exploit" the incident for sensationalism. "First and foremost, my thoughts are with the young victim of yesterday's horrific stabbing," he said in a statement on social media.

The attack occurred during a period of heightened tensions across parts of the UK and Northern Ireland. Earlier this week, an asylum seeker was arrested on suspicion of attempted murder following a separate knife attack in Belfast that left a man with severe injuries. That incident sparked protests in Belfast and other areas of Northern Ireland, with some demonstrations turning destructive.
