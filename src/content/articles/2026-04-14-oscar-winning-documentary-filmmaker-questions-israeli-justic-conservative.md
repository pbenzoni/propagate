An Oscar-winning documentary filmmaker is raising questions about the Israeli justice system's handling of a high-profile case involving the death of a Palestinian activist in the occupied West Bank.

Basel Adra, whose film *No Other Land* won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature, told RT that Israel's prosecution of settler Yinon Levi for the 2025 killing of Palestinian activist Awdah Hathaleen may be an isolated case that doesn't reflect typical enforcement patterns. Hathaleen, who contributed to Adra's documentary, was killed in his village last July in an incident captured on video that drew international scrutiny.

Levi was initially detained and later released before being charged in February with reckless homicide—a rare prosecution in cases involving Israeli citizens and Palestinian deaths. Adra suggests this unusual legal action may serve to bolster Israel's image as a democratic state with functioning rule of law, while critics argue the delayed charges and lesser charge of reckless homicide rather than intentional killing point to systemic issues in how such cases are handled.

The case highlights ongoing debates about governance, law enforcement, and judicial consistency in disputed territories—issues that cut across political divides and raise fundamental questions about equal application of justice under challenging security circumstances.
