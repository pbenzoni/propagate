President Donald Trump declared that Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu will have "no choice" but to accept any nuclear agreement the U.S. reaches with Iran, emphasizing his own authority in Middle East diplomacy. "I call the shots. I call all the shots. He doesn't call the shots," Trump told the Financial Times in a Sunday interview.

The remarks came shortly after Iran launched a missile barrage at Israel in response to Israeli airstrikes on Beirut that killed at least two people and wounded 20 others. Unlike typical Israeli operations in densely populated areas, this strike on a residential building in Beirut's southern suburbs occurred without advance warning, according to Lebanon's Health Ministry.

Trump insisted the Iranian attack would not derail his pursuit of a nuclear deal with Tehran, despite Iran's stated requirement that any agreement include a permanent Israeli ceasefire in Lebanon. The President's comments follow reports of a contentious phone call in which he allegedly told Netanyahu, "You're f**king crazy. You'd be in prison if it weren't for me. Everybody hates you now," according to Axios. Trump later confirmed the call occurred without disputing its characterization.

The exchange highlights growing tensions in the U.S.-Israel relationship as Netanyahu's government continues daily strikes in Lebanon despite multiple U.S.-brokered ceasefires, including one that took effect last week. The pattern underscores questions about Washington's ability to restrain its closest Middle East ally, even as the administration pursues broader regional diplomatic initiatives.
