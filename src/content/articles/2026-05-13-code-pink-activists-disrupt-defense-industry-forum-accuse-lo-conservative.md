Anti-war activists from Code Pink disrupted a Politico security forum in Washington, DC on Tuesday, protesting what they called war profiteering by defense contractor Lockheed Martin. The 'Security Summit' brought together US officials, lawmakers, defense executives, and foreign figures to discuss military priorities, Iran, cybersecurity, and AI warfare.

Protesters interrupted multiple live panels, accusing Lockheed Martin and government officials of profiting from Middle East conflicts. "This is not about security. This is about death and destruction. This is about profit!" one activist shouted while disrupting Representative Adam Smith, ranking member of the House Armed Services Committee. The protesters held signs reading "Lockheed Loves War" and called for an end to military operations in Gaza.

An Iranian-American activist confronted Reza Pahlavi, the exiled son of Iran's last shah, over his support for US-Israeli military action against Iran. "Where were you when our people were pulling their children out from under the rubble of the bombs you invited into our country?" the protester demanded. At least four protesters were removed from the event.

Lockheed Martin, the world's largest defense contractor, receives more than $60 billion annually in Pentagon contracts. The company supplies F-15 and F-35 jets, Hellfire missiles, and other weapons systems used in Middle East operations. Lockheed has also significantly increased production to supply Ukraine with Patriot air defense systems, HIMARS launchers, ATACMS missiles, F-16 fighter jets, and Javelin anti-tank missiles through US aid programs.
