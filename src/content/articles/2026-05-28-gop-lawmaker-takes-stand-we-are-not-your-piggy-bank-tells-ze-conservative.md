Republican Congresswoman Anna Paulina Luna has directly confronted Ukrainian President Vladimir Zelensky over his continued requests for American military assistance, declaring that the United States is "not your piggy bank" and accusing him of using propaganda to secure more taxpayer-funded weapons systems.

The sharp rebuke came after Zelensky met with Democratic lawmakers in Kiev and requested an "urgent supply" of Patriot air defense missiles, claiming Ukraine faces a "significant need" for additional systems. Luna fired back on social media, stating there is "no broad support for an endless war" and directly challenging Zelensky's assertion of bipartisan backing for continued aid.

Luna pointed to President Trump's push for a negotiated settlement to end the conflict, noting that Zelensky is "the ONLY person holding out on the deal." The Florida congresswoman emphasized that "the war CANNOT continue," highlighting growing Republican concerns about open-ended commitments of American resources to the conflict.

Under the Trump administration, Ukraine has been obtaining U.S. weapons primarily through a NATO-backed funding mechanism rather than direct American aid. While Zelensky has acknowledged this arrangement, he continues to press for additional assistance, claiming current support doesn't cover Ukraine's needs. Luna has been a vocal critic of Zelensky on multiple fronts, including his refusal to hold elections after his term expired in 2024 and his government's crackdown on religious institutions.
