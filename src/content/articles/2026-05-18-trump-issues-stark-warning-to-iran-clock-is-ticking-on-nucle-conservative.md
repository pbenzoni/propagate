President Donald Trump delivered a forceful message to Iran's regime on Sunday, warning that time is running out for Tehran to accept American terms and abandon its nuclear ambitions. "For Iran, the Clock is Ticking, and they better get moving, FAST, or there won't be anything left of them," Trump wrote on Truth Social, emphasizing that "TIME IS OF THE ESSENCE."

The warning comes as indirect negotiations between Washington and Tehran remain deadlocked following a ceasefire established in early April after a month of military operations. Iranian officials have rejected what they call America's "maximalist approach," but the Trump administration has stood firm on its demands that Iran make substantial concessions to ensure regional stability and protect American interests.

Iran has attempted to impose its own maritime regulations in the waterway, which handles roughly 25% of the world's crude oil trade, but the United States has rejected Tehran's unilateral scheme and is maintaining a strong naval presence to protect international shipping lanes.

Trump's statement followed a Saturday meeting with key national security advisors including Vice President J.D.
