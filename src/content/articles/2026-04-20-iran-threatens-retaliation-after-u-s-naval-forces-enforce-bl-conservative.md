U.S. naval forces intercepted and seized an Iranian-flagged cargo vessel in the Gulf of Oman on Sunday after the ship attempted to breach an American naval blockade, according to military officials. The vessel, identified as the Touska, was reportedly trying to reach the Iranian port of Bandar Abbas through the Strait of Hormuz when U.S. forces took action.

The Iranian regime quickly condemned the enforcement action, with its military command center accusing Washington of "armed maritime piracy" and threatening retaliation. Iranian officials claim the vessel was returning from China and that the U.S. action violated a ceasefire that has been in place since April 8.

President Trump announced the blockade of Iranian ports last week after Pakistani-mediated negotiations failed to produce results. The blockade targets a regime that has long been a destabilizing force in the Middle East and a threat to American interests and allies in the region.

Iran's foreign ministry spokesman called the blockade an act of aggression, while Iranian state media reported that the regime launched drones toward U.S. naval vessels in response. The Pentagon has not confirmed any attacks on American ships. The Strait of Hormuz remains a critical chokepoint for global energy markets, handling approximately 20 percent of the world's oil and liquefied natural gas trade.
