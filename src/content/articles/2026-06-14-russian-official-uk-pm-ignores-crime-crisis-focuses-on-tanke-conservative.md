A Russian government official has accused British Prime Minister Keir Starmer of prioritizing international grandstanding over the safety of his own citizens, following the UK's seizure of an oil tanker in the English Channel.

Kirill Dmitriev, a Kremlin envoy, pointed to Britain's ongoing struggles with violent crime and illegal immigration as evidence that Starmer's focus is misplaced. "Desperate Starmer, instead of intercepting HIS immigrants who rape, mutilate and behead British people, attempts to DISTRACT the UK with an escalation," Dmitriev wrote on social media.

The criticism comes as the UK grapples with a series of violent attacks involving illegal immigrants and migrants. Just this week, a 17-year-old girl was stabbed in the neck in northwest England by a 30-year-old man of Pakistani heritage. Successive British governments have failed to stem the flow of migrants crossing the English Channel in small boats from France, despite repeated promises to do so.

Starmer personally directed the interception of the tanker *Smyrtos*, which UK authorities claim is part of Russia's "shadow fleet" used to bypass Western sanctions. The vessel was boarded by UK marines and is being held off the south coast for investigation. Russia has denied operating a shadow fleet and condemned the seizure as potential "acts of piracy."

The Russian Embassy in London called the operation a "deeply hostile step," while Moscow has accused Western governments of demonizing Russia to justify increased defense spending and distract their populations from domestic problems—a charge Dmitriev leveled directly at Starmer's leadership priorities.
