In a troubling development that raises concerns about the weaponization of national security apparatus, White House counterterrorism director Sebastian Gorka has suggested that prominent critics of President Trump—including former Fox News host Tucker Carlson and nationalist commentator Nick Fuentes—should be considered "left-wing extremists" and potential threats.

The classification comes as part of the administration's newly released National Strategy for Counterterrorism, which identifies "Violent Left-Wing Extremists, including Anarchists and Anti-Fascists" as one of three primary terror threats facing the United States. Notably absent from the strategy: any mention of right-wing extremism, despite well-documented concerns from civil rights organizations and security experts about violence from far-right movements.

When pressed by Breitbart about whether he views "right-wing extremism" as a threat, Gorka performed rhetorical gymnastics to reclassify Trump's critics. "I'm not sure that Nick Fuentes or Tucker Carlson are conservatives," Gorka said, suggesting that by stripping them of conservative credentials, they could be reframed as the type of left-wing threats his strategy targets. The logic appears designed to justify surveillance or action against political dissent.

The Carlson-Trump relationship deteriorated sharply over the former president's handling of Middle East policy, particularly regarding military action against Iran and support for Israel's regional operations. Carlson publicly criticized Trump's Easter Sunday threats against Iranian civilian infrastructure as "vile on every level," asking "Who do you think you are? You're tweeting out the f-word on Easter morning." Trump responded by calling Carlson a "flailing fool" and declaring him an enemy of his political movement.

Civil liberties advocates have expressed alarm at the administration's framework, which identifies "anti-Americanism, anti-capitalism, and anti-Christianity" as indicators of potential terroristic activities—definitions critics say could encompass vast swaths of legitimate political dissent and protest.
