More than 500 British police officers conducted a major raid on the headquarters of an Islamic sect in Cheshire, arresting nine members of the Ahmadi Religion of Peace and Light—a fringe movement that relocated from Sweden to Britain in 2021.

Cheshire Police launched their investigation in March 2024 after receiving reports of serious sexual offenses, forced marriage, and modern slavery allegedly committed in 2023 against a female member of the group. The pre-dawn operation targeted three locations, including Webb House, a former orphanage now serving as the sect's compound. Authorities detained six men and three women holding American, Mexican, Italian, Spanish, Egyptian, and Swedish citizenship.

The secretive movement, founded in the late 1990s by followers of Shia Islam, claims roughly 7,000 members across more than 40 countries. Swedish immigration authorities previously ordered deportation of 69 members before the group's UK relocation. The British Home Office is now investigating the sect's use of skilled worker visas.
