President Donald Trump confirmed this week that he confronted Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu in blunt terms over Israel's military operations in Lebanon, telling the *New York Post*'s podcast that he called the Israeli leader "f***ing crazy" during a heated phone call.

The president explained his frustration stemmed from ongoing strikes in Lebanon that threatened to derail delicate negotiations with Iran and undermine a fragile regional ceasefire. "I was a little bit perturbed at his constantly fighting with Lebanon," Trump said. "At some point I said, 'We gotta stop this. We gotta stop it.'" Despite the tough language, Trump emphasized he maintains a "very good relationship" with Netanyahu and pushed back against critics suggesting Israel was manipulating U.S. foreign policy, calling such accusations the work of "the enemy."

Israel has ramped up operations in Lebanon in recent days, targeting what it describes as Hezbollah commanders and infrastructure. Israeli forces have advanced into southern Lebanon, including capturing the historic Beaufort Castle overlooking the Litani River. Lebanese authorities report over 3,500 casualties since early March from Israeli strikes.

The escalation has complicated Trump's broader Middle East strategy.
