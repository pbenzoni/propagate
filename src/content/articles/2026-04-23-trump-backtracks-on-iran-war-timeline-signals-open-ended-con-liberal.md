President Donald Trump has reversed course on his previous assurances about quickly resolving the ongoing conflict with Iran, telling Fox News on Wednesday that there is "no time frame" for ending military operations.

The statement marks a significant departure from Trump's earlier rhetoric. Shortly after the US and Israel launched attacks on Iran on February 28, the president claimed the fighting would last "four to five weeks." Throughout the past month, he repeatedly assured Americans the war would be over "very soon."

Trump also dismissed suggestions that political considerations were driving his approach to the conflict. "People say I want to get it over because of the midterms, not true," he stated, addressing speculation that electoral pressures might influence the administration's military strategy.

The lack of a clear timeline for disengagement raises concerns about mission creep and the potential for prolonged military involvement without congressional authorization or public debate about objectives. Critics have long warned about the dangers of open-ended military commitments that lack clearly defined goals and exit strategies.
