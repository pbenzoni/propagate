British Prime Minister Keir Starmer accused Elon Musk of "whipping up division" after the tech entrepreneur criticized UK police for their treatment of Henry Nowak, an 18-year-old university student who was fatally stabbed in December. The case has sparked widespread outrage after body camera footage revealed officers handcuffing and ignoring the dying teenager's pleas for help.

Nowak was stabbed five times by Vickrum Singh Digwa, 23, who falsely told police he had been the victim of a racist attack. The released footage shows officers dismissing Nowak's repeated statements that he had been stabbed and couldn't breathe, instead dragging and restraining him. The teen later lost consciousness and died. Digwa was convicted of murder and sentenced to life in prison with a minimum 21-year term.

Musk has posted multiple times about the case on X, urging followers to share the footage and accusing police of treating Nowak differently based on his ethnicity. "Send the video to everyone you know showing how heinously Nowak was treated by the police in his dying moments," Musk wrote, noting that mainstream media outlets that extensively covered similar cases in America have remained largely silent about Nowak's death.

After meeting with Nowak's family at Downing Street, Starmer attacked Musk for "interfering in our politics" and attempting to create division. The prime minister's response comes amid growing questions about whether police response was influenced by the false racism accusation. Reform UK leader Nigel Farage said the case demonstrates Britain is living in a "two-tier culture" where "a false accusation of racism counted higher at that moment than someone that was dying."
