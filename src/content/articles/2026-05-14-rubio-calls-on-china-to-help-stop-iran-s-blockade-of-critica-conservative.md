The United States is seeking China's cooperation to pressure Iran into ending its blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, Secretary of State Marco Rubio said ahead of high-level talks in Beijing.

Speaking to Fox News host Sean Hannity aboard Air Force One, Rubio outlined the administration's strategy to enlist Chinese help in resolving the Middle East crisis. "It's in their interest to resolve this," Rubio explained. "We hope to convince them to play a more active role in getting Iran to walk away from what they're doing now and trying to do now in the Persian Gulf."

Rubio made a direct economic appeal to Beijing, arguing that the Iranian blockade threatens China's export-driven economy. "Economies are melting down because of this crisis in the Strait. They're going to be buying less Chinese product and Chinese exports are going to drop precipitously," the Secretary of State said.

President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping are scheduled to hold discussions on Thursday, with the Strait of Hormuz blockade and Taiwan among the expected topics. The Strait is a critical chokepoint for global oil shipments.

China has previously criticized U.S. military action against Iran and declined to comply with American sanctions targeting Chinese companies accused of aiding Tehran. Beijing has ordered domestic businesses not to follow the U.S. restrictions.
