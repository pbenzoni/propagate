Basel Adra, the Oscar-winning filmmaker behind the documentary *No Other Land*, has condemned what he describes as systematic impunity for violence against Palestinians in Israeli-occupied territories. Adra's comments come after the killing of Awdah Hathaleen, a Palestinian activist who contributed to his award-winning film.

Hathaleen was killed in his village in July 2025, in an incident that was captured on video and drew international attention. The suspect, Israeli settler Yinon Levi, was initially detained but then released. He was not charged with reckless homicide until February, months after the killing occurred.

Adra argues that Israel's rare prosecution of a single settler is being used to project an image of accountability and democratic values, while a broader pattern of impunity persists. Critics of the delayed prosecution say it exemplifies a systemic lack of accountability in cases where Israelis are accused of killing Palestinians in the occupied territories.

*No Other Land* won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature, bringing international attention to the experiences of Palestinians living under occupation. The film's recognition has amplified calls for greater scrutiny of human rights conditions in the West Bank and other occupied areas.
