The acknowledgment raises serious questions about accountability and the risk to civilian populations when weapons systems are deployed before rigorous testing is complete.

The PrSM, manufactured by defense contractor Lockheed Martin, completed its prototype phase only last year and was fielded without a full testing cycle. According to media reports and weapons experts, the missiles were likely involved in a strike on the Iranian city of Lamerd that killed 21 people at a school and sports facility on February 28.
