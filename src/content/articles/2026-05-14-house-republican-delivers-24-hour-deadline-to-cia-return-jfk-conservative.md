Representative Anna Paulina Luna has issued a stark ultimatum to the Central Intelligence Agency: return 40 boxes of critical documents on the JFK assassination and Cold War-era government experiments within 24 hours, or face congressional subpoena. The Florida Republican's demand comes after a CIA whistleblower testified that the agency seized the files while they were being processed for declassification under President Trump's executive order.

According to testimony before a Senate committee, CIA whistleblower James Erdman III revealed that the agency took back documents related to President Kennedy's 1963 assassination and the notorious MKUltra program—a Cold War project that subjected American citizens to LSD and psychological experiments without their knowledge or consent. The files had been under the authority of Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard, who was tasked by President Trump with declassifying records related to the assassinations of JFK, RFK, and Martin Luther King Jr.

Rep. Luna suggested that elements within the CIA are "actively undermining" the President's transparency directive, warning of "punitive action incoming" if the documents are not returned. While DNI spokesperson Olivia Coleman disputed characterizations of a "raid," she notably did not deny that the CIA had taken possession of the documents. Intelligence sources later confirmed the removal occurred during the 2025 government shutdown.

The Kennedy assassination has long been a flashpoint for Americans' distrust of federal agencies. A 2023 Gallup poll found that 65% of Americans believe JFK was killed as part of a conspiracy rather than by a lone gunman. While no declassified document has proven CIA involvement in the killing, investigations have established that the agency concealed critical information, including that it had been tracking Lee Harvey Oswald before the assassination. The fight over these documents represents a broader battle between congressional oversight and intelligence agencies' resistance to transparency—a struggle that resonates deeply with Americans who believe their government has hidden the truth for over six decades.
