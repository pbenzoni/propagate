At least two people were killed and several others injured when a vehicle drove into a crowd on a busy shopping street in Leipzig, Germany, on Monday, according to local media reports.

The incident occurred on Grimmaische Strasse, a central pedestrian shopping area in the eastern German city. Local broadcaster Radio Leipzig reported that a damaged Volkswagen SUV was seen in the pedestrian zone. Leipzig Mayor Burkhard Jung confirmed the preliminary death toll and stated that the surrounding area had been secured.

Police in Saxony have detained the driver, according to Deutsche Welle. Authorities have not released the driver's identity, and it remains unclear whether the incident was deliberate or accidental. An investigation is ongoing.
