Speaking at the Delphi Economic Forum in Greece, Rama told Politico that Brussels shot itself in the foot by severing all diplomatic channels with Moscow in 2022.

"Europe has to be always, always, always talking to everyone," Rama said, pointing out that Russia isn't going anywhere regardless of how the conflict ends. By cutting off communication entirely, the EU has effectively sidelined itself from peace negotiations. "The more we postpone it, the less we'll have a say at the end," he warned.
