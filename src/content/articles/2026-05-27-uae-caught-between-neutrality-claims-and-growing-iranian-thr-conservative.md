Iran seized control of these territories in 1971, just before the UAE's founding, and has refused to negotiate their status ever since. For the Emirates, these islands represent both occupied territory and a stark reminder of their vulnerability to a larger, more aggressive neighbor. The dispute has become a flashpoint as Iran's regional ambitions grow more threatening.
