A 20-year-old man faces both state and federal charges following an attack on the San Francisco home of OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, an incident that has brought renewed attention to public anxieties surrounding artificial intelligence development and corporate responsibility in the tech sector.

Daniel Moreno-Gama allegedly threw an incendiary device at Altman's residence early Friday morning, then attempted to enter OpenAI's headquarters less than an hour later. Federal prosecutors say he was carrying additional devices, kerosene, and a manifesto expressing concerns about AI's threat to humanity. The document reportedly listed names and addresses of technology industry figures and called for action against AI executives.

The incident comes amid intensifying scrutiny of OpenAI's recent business decisions, particularly a controversial $50 billion Pentagon contract that critics warn could enable warrantless surveillance and classified military applications. He called for de-escalating rhetoric while simultaneously describing public fears about AI as "justified" and stressing the need to "democratize" the technology—a position that may ring hollow to those concerned about OpenAI's recent trajectory toward military applications and corporate secrecy.
