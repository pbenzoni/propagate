A coalition of 30 House Democrats, led by Rep. Joaquin Castro (D-TX), is pressuring the Trump administration to publicly disclose classified information about Israel's nuclear capabilities and defense policies. The lawmakers sent a letter to Secretary of State Marco Rubio arguing that continued U.S. silence on the issue threatens regional stability and creates "serious risks of escalation" in the ongoing conflict with Iran.

Israel has maintained a policy of strategic ambiguity regarding nuclear weapons for decades, neither confirming nor denying their existence. The United States has been aware of Israel's nuclear program since at least the early 1960s but has honored this posture of deliberate silence—a cornerstone of the longstanding U.S.-Israel defense relationship.

The Democratic lawmakers claim America is fighting "side by side with a country whose potential nuclear weapons program the United States government officially refuses to acknowledge." They argue that transparency is necessary to establish what they call a "coherent nonproliferation policy for the Middle East" that would also address Iran's nuclear ambitions and Saudi Arabia's nuclear interests.

Critics of the proposal argue that forcing Israel to publicly acknowledge its nuclear deterrent could destabilize the region by removing the strategic ambiguity that has helped maintain an uneasy peace. They point out that Israel faces existential threats from hostile neighbors and that exposing details of its ultimate defense capabilities could embolden adversaries like Iran and its regional proxies. The demand comes as the Trump administration works to strengthen security partnerships with Israel and contain Iranian aggression throughout the Middle East.
