President Donald Trump launched a sharp attack on German Chancellor Friedrich Merz this week after the European leader publicly criticized the administration's handling of military operations against Iran. Merz had stated on Monday that the U.S. was being "humiliated" by Iran and accused the Trump administration of lacking a coherent strategy in the escalating conflict.

In a Thursday post on Truth Social, Trump dismissed Merz's concerns and suggested the German chancellor should instead focus on "ending the war with Russia/Ukraine" and addressing domestic issues including immigration and energy policy.
