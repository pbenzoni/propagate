Italian police have arrested five illegal migrants in connection with the alleged kidnapping, drugging, and brutal assault of a 32-year-old Colombian tourist in Rome, according to a Thursday statement from law enforcement authorities.

The victim was reportedly abducted last week after dining alone at a restaurant in Italy's capital. Police say she was approached by a man after mentioning she was looking to buy hashish. Investigators allege the man lured her to a secluded area where she was forced into a van and taken to an abandoned building housing at least 22 illegal migrants. Her phone and identification documents were stolen, according to authorities.

Over the next three days, police say the woman was repeatedly assaulted by multiple men, kept under the influence of drugs, and threatened with death. She managed to escape when her alleged attackers became distracted and, half-naked and in shock, flagged down a passerby who contacted police. Hospital doctors found injuries consistent with her account and traces of narcotics in her system, the police statement said.

Authorities raided the abandoned building and found 22 illegal migrants at the site. Five men were arrested on suspicion of involvement in the alleged crimes, while 11 others are reportedly set for deportation. According to the Daily Mail, all five suspects are of African origin.

Italy has become a primary entry point for migrants crossing the Mediterranean from Africa, with U.N. data showing more than 66,000 arrivals by sea over the past year. The issue of migrant-related crime has become a significant concern for Italian citizens, with foreign nationals accounting for approximately 43% of reported sexual assaults in 2024 despite comprising only about 9% of the population, according to Italian Foreign Ministry data analyzed by Il Sole 24 Ore.
