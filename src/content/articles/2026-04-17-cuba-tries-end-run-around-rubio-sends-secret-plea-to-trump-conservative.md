The communist Cuban regime attempted to open a secret back-channel to President Donald Trump last week, dispatching a private courier to hand-deliver a sealed letter directly to the White House, the Wall Street Journal reported Thursday. The gambit was designed to circumvent Secretary of State Marco Rubio, a staunch advocate of maximum pressure on the Castro dictatorship.

According to sources cited by the Journal, the letter was orchestrated by Raul Rodriguez Castro, grandson of 94-year-old former Cuban strongman Raul Castro. The message bore an official government seal and proposed economic deals and sanctions relief, while warning that Havana fears a possible U.S. military intervention. The courier, a luxury tourism entrepreneur based in Havana, was stopped by security at Miami International Airport, where officers confiscated the letter and sent him back to Cuba.

The attempted workaround of Rubio—a son of Cuban immigrants and Washington's most vocal proponent of regime change in Havana—was called "downright foolish" by Ricardo Herrero of the Washington-based Cuba Study Group. The back-channel bid comes as Cuba suffers its worst economic crisis in decades, compounded by severe fuel shortages and recurring blackouts.

President Trump has previously labeled Cuba "a failing nation" and hinted at potential U.S. action, recently stating the U.S. "may stop by Cuba" after operations in Iran conclude. Havana has warned it is prepared for any American intervention, though the regime's desperation is increasingly evident as its economic lifelines dwindle.
