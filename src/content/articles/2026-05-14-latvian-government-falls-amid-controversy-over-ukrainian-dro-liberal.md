Latvia's Prime Minister Evika Silina resigned Thursday amid a political crisis triggered by Ukrainian drones striking an oil depot near the Russian border last week. The incident, which caused no casualties, exposed deep divisions within the NATO member's governing coalition over how to respond when Ukraine's military operations spill across allied borders.

The crisis escalated rapidly after Defense Minister Andris Spruds resigned over the weekend, saying he wanted to keep the military out of political disputes. Spruds had characterized the drone strike as "regrettable but understandable" given Ukraine's defensive war against Russian aggression. However, his stance divided coalition partners, with opposition lawmakers preparing a no-confidence vote that appeared certain to succeed.

At her resignation announcement, Silina criticized what she called "political jealousy and narrow party interests" for undermining the government during a critical period.
