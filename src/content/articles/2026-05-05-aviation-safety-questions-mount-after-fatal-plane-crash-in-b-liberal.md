A tragic aviation accident in Belo Horizonte, Brazil claimed three lives on Monday when a small single-engine aircraft crashed into a residential building shortly after takeoff. The 1979 Embraer 721C plane, carrying five people, struck the stairwell of a four-story building in the Silveira neighborhood minutes after departing Pampulha Airport.

The pilot, Wellington Oliveira, 34, had reported difficulties during takeoff to airport control before the crash. Also killed were Fernando Moreira Souto, 36, and a third passenger who initially survived but later died from injuries. Two other passengers remain hospitalized in serious condition. Remarkably, no residents of the building were injured, as the aircraft struck between floors rather than directly impacting apartments.

Brazilian authorities, including the Center for Investigation and Prevention of Aeronautical Accidents, have launched a comprehensive investigation into what caused the mechanical difficulties that led to this preventable tragedy.
