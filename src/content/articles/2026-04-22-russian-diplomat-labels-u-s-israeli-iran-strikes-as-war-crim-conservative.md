Russia's Ambassador to Iran, Aleksey Dedov, has publicly condemned recent U.S. and Israeli military strikes on Iran as war crimes, following a visit to a destroyed hospital in Tehran. The diplomat made the statement while touring damaged areas of the Iranian capital alongside other foreign ambassadors.

According to reports from the scene, the hospital was struck without advance warning while patients and medical staff were inside. The extent of casualties remains unclear. "It's a horrible thing to see," Dedov told reporters while assessing the destruction. "This is evidence of an inhumane approach by Israel and the US… it's very clearly a war crime."

Tehran Province Governor Mohammad-Sadegh Motamedian organized the diplomatic tour to allow foreign representatives to witness the aftermath firsthand. He stated the visit aimed to highlight what he characterized as Western "silence" regarding American and Israeli military actions in the region.

The incident raises questions about rules of engagement and the protection of civilian infrastructure during military operations. Critics of the strikes point to international humanitarian law provisions that require precautions to minimize harm to non-combatants and protected facilities like hospitals.
