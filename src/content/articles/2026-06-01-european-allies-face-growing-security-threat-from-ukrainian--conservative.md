The response from Brussels? Deflect blame toward Russia rather than hold Kiev accountable for its own wayward equipment.

Lithuanian President Gitanas Nauseda has drawn a firm line, insisting his nation's sovereignty must be respected and that it won't tolerate unauthorized military operations within its borders. Yet when Ukrainian drones continue crossing into allied airspace—whether through operator error or technical malfunction—European leaders have proven reluctant to demand answers from a government receiving billions in Western aid.

The consequences have been real.
