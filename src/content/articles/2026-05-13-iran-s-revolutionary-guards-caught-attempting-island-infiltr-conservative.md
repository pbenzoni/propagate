The incident, which occurred on May 1, resulted in a Kuwaiti soldier being wounded during a clash with security forces.

According to Kuwait's Interior Ministry, the suspects were detained aboard a fishing boat that had been chartered for "hostile actions" against the nation. During interrogation, all four individuals admitted their affiliation with Iran's IRGC, according to the state news agency KUNA. Kuwait's Foreign Ministry condemned what it called a "flagrant violation" of its sovereignty and summoned Iran's ambassador to deliver a formal protest.

Bubiyan Island sits at a critical juncture near the Iraqi border and vital shipping routes in the northern Gulf. Beyond its proximity to major oilfields, the island hosts American military infrastructure and is part of a port development project linked to China's Belt and Road Initiative. The attempted infiltration underscores Iran's continued pattern of aggressive behavior in the region, particularly targeting areas where U.S. forces are stationed.

Tehran predictably rejected the accusations, claiming the men were innocent sailors whose vessel accidentally entered Kuwaiti waters due to navigation equipment failure.
