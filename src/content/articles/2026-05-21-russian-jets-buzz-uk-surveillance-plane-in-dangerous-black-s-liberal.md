The UK Defense Ministry released footage Wednesday showing Russian fighter jets conducting what officials called a dangerously close intercept of a British reconnaissance aircraft over the Black Sea. The video appears to show two Russian jets maneuvering near the unarmed plane, with one flying close enough to trigger emergency systems aboard the aircraft.

Defense Secretary John Healey condemned the incident as "dangerous and unacceptable behavior" toward an unarmed aircraft operating in international airspace. According to the ministry, a Russian Su-27 fighter flew as close as six meters from the nose of the RAF Rivet Joint surveillance plane, which is equipped with advanced electronic monitoring systems. Despite the aggressive maneuvers, the British crew was able to complete its mission without incident.

The Black Sea has become an arena of heightened military tensions since Russia's invasion of Ukraine. The region has seen increased Western surveillance operations monitoring the conflict, as well as Ukrainian drone strikes and attacks using Western-supplied missiles targeting Russian infrastructure. Moscow has not officially commented on the UK's allegations.

Such dangerous intercepts raise serious concerns about aviation safety in international airspace and the risk of miscalculation that could lead to broader escalation. Russia has consistently maintained that its pilots follow international law, though Western officials argue these close encounters violate established norms for safely approaching military aircraft.
