Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has disclosed that he was treated for early-stage prostate cancer, revealing he kept the diagnosis confidential for two months while Israel was engaged in military operations against Iran.

"You already know me. When I'm given information in time about a potential danger, I want to address it immediately," Netanyahu wrote on social media. "This is true on the national level and also on the personal level."
