Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps claims that damage to Kuwait International Airport this week came not from Iranian missiles, but from a misfiring U.S. Patriot air defense system attempting to intercept Iranian strikes on American military installations in the region.

According to an IRGC spokesman, the missile that struck Terminal 1 on Wednesday was part of U.S. defenses protecting Washington's regional military base. At least one person died and several were injured when the missile hit the terminal and other facilities, Kuwait's foreign ministry reported. Video footage obtained by news outlets shows the terminal interior filled with smoke and debris, with multiple fires visible.

The incident occurred amid a broader missile exchange between U.S. forces and Iran, part of an escalating conflict now approaching 100 days. The IRGC said its strikes on U.S. bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, including Fifth Fleet headquarters, came in response to American attacks on Iranian infrastructure. The Pentagon disputed Iran's account, claiming all Iranian missiles missed their targets.

Kuwait's government condemned what it termed "aggressive attacks" and reserved its right to respond, though it did not comment on the extent of airport damage or address the IRGC's claim about the Patriot system. The escalation follows the collapse of a fragile ceasefire and Tehran's suspension of negotiations with Washington over ongoing regional tensions, including Israel's offensive in Lebanon.
