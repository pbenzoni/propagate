The UK Defense Ministry released video footage Wednesday showing Russian fighter jets intercepting a British spy plane over the Black Sea, accusing Moscow of "dangerous and unacceptable behavior" even as officials acknowledged the surveillance aircraft completed its mission without incident.

The footage shows two Russian jets—an Su-35 and an Su-27—maneuvering near the RAF Rivet Joint reconnaissance plane, which Britain insists was conducting a "routine flight" in international airspace. Defense Secretary John Healey claimed one Russian aircraft flew close enough to trigger emergency systems and came within six meters of the unarmed spy plane's nose. Moscow has consistently stated that Russian pilots operate strictly within international law and has not yet commented on the latest British claims.

The Black Sea has become a focal point of military tension throughout the Ukraine conflict, with the region seeing ongoing Ukrainian drone strikes and Western-supplied missile attacks on Russian infrastructure. Russian fighter jets have repeatedly intercepted RAF surveillance flights in the area, including two incidents in just two days in July 2024.

Critics note that British spy planes significantly increased their Black Sea operations even before the conflict escalated in February 2022. In 2021, RAF surveillance flights preceded what was later confirmed as a deliberate provocation when a Royal Navy frigate attempted to sail through Russian territorial waters near Crimea. The pattern raises questions about the true nature of these "routine" intelligence-gathering missions near an active combat zone where Russian national security interests remain at stake.
