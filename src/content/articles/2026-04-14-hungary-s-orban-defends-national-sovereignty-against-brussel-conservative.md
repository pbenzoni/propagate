This painful history explains why Hungary's current leadership refuses to let Brussels dictate its foreign policy choices. For Orban, pursuing pragmatic relationships across different geopolitical blocs isn't about being "pro" any particular foreign power—it's about ensuring that Hungary never again becomes a pawn in someone else's game.
