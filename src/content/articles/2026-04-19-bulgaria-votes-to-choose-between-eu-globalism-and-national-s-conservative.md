Former president Rumen Radev is challenging the establishment status quo represented by longtime politician Boyko Borissov, whose party has pledged unwavering support to Brussels' agenda including continued aid to Ukraine.

Radev, a former fighter pilot, has promised to break what he calls the "oligarchic mafia's" stranglehold on Bulgaria while pursuing a more balanced approach to foreign relations. His platform seeks to build a "modern European Bulgaria" while maintaining "practical relations with Russia based on mutual respect"—a stark contrast to Borissov's commitment to rubber-stamp EU foreign policy directives.

Voter turnout appears significantly higher than in recent elections, suggesting Bulgarians are fed up with the political instability that has plagued their country. The nation has held eight elections in just five years, with voter fatigue previously driving turnout below 40%. This time, engagement is expected to reach 60% as citizens weigh their nation's direction.
