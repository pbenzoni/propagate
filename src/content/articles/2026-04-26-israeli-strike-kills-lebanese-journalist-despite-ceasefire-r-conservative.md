The two journalists were traveling through the village of al-Tiri when Israeli drones struck nearby vehicles. Both women were wearing clearly marked press gear, according to colleagues. "She told me: 'Zeinab, I'm burning!' I grabbed her by her jacket and pulled her toward me," Faraj recalled from her hospital bed, breaking down as she described trying to shield her colleague from the flames.
