Eight children lost their lives Sunday morning in Shreveport, Louisiana, when a domestic dispute escalated into a horrific shooting spree that spanned multiple locations across the city. Police responded to reports of a domestic disturbance in the 300 block of West 79th Street just after 6:00 AM local time.

The violence claimed ten victims in total, with eight fatalities—all children ranging in age from just 18 months to 15 years old. According to Police Chief Wayne Smith, the only survivors were two adult women, one of whom remains in life-threatening condition. One woman is believed to have been in a relationship with the suspect, and some of the children were related to him. "It's an extensive scene unlike anything most of us have ever seen," Chief Smith said.

The suspect fled the initial scene and attempted to carjack a vehicle before law enforcement pursued him. Officers ultimately shot and killed the suspect following the chase, ending the threat to the community. No officers were injured during the incident. Police spokesman Christopher Bordelon confirmed the violence occurred across three different scenes, with at least one victim fleeing to a neighboring residence.

Shreveport Mayor Tom Arceneaux called it "a tragic situation – maybe the worst tragic situation we've ever had." Authorities have not yet formally released the suspect's identity or provided additional details about the motive beyond the domestic nature of the incident. The investigation remains ongoing.
