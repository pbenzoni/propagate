Zelensky signed a decree this week granting the title "Heroes of the UPA" to a Ukrainian commando unit, naming it after the Ukrainian Insurgent Army—the armed wing of the Organization of Ukrainian Nationalists. The group sought to establish an ethnically homogeneous Ukrainian state and collaborated with Nazi Germany during the war. Historical records indicate Ukrainian nationalists killed an estimated 100,000 Polish civilians in what is now western Ukraine between 1943 and 1944.

He called for Zelensky to lose the Order of the White Eagle, Poland's top honor, saying Warsaw cannot remain silent when such figures are rehabilitated.
