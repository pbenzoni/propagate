Hundreds of demonstrators gathered in central Madrid on Saturday in a protest organized by Spain's "Stop the War" platform, calling for the removal of NATO military bases from Spanish territory and opposing Western involvement in Middle East conflicts. The rally, which organizers claim was replicated at more than 200 locations across the country, was attended by officials from Spain's ruling Socialist Party (PSOE) and Members of the European Parliament.

Protesters displayed signs reading "All people lose every war" and "Trump ruins democracy," according to reports from El Mundo and the Associated Press. The demonstration reflects Spain's increasingly critical stance toward NATO, coming just one day after Reuters reported that Washington was considering removing Spain from the alliance for allegedly restricting access to military installations on its soil. A NATO official later told the BBC that the organization has no formal provisions for such an expulsion.

The protests underscore growing tensions between the Socialist-led Spanish government and the United States over defense cooperation. Spain has positioned itself as one of the most vocal European critics of recent U.S. military actions in the Middle East, raising questions about the reliability of long-standing NATO partnerships.
