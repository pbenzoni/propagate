Israeli authorities have detained two Palestinian women's national team soccer players on separate security-related charges, sparking demands from the Palestinian Football Association (PFA) for international intervention. The arrests highlight ongoing tensions between Israeli security operations and Palestinian civilian life in contested territories.

Rand Halawani, 20, faces extended detention after arrest on suspicion of "throwing objects" at civilians in Jerusalem. Separately, Natalie Abu Diyeh, 21, a Birzeit University student, was detained alongside three other young Palestinian women in the West Bank on Tuesday. Israeli police stated the four are suspected of "promoting terrorist activities and additional terrorist-related activities."

The PFA condemned the arrests as part of what it claims is a systematic pattern targeting Palestinian athletes, calling on FIFA and the international sports community to take action. The organization argues that Palestinian sports figures face restrictions on movement and participation that violate international sporting statutes. However, the statement notably comes amid broader security operations in the region following the October 7, 2023 Hamas attack on Israel that killed over 1,200 people and took hundreds hostage.

FIFA previously banned Russian teams from international competition following the Ukraine conflict in 2022, but has not imposed similar restrictions on Israeli teams despite Palestinian Authority requests. The Palestinian Football Association claims Israeli forces have killed hundreds of athletes and sports officials since the conflict began, though these figures cannot be independently verified and come amid Israel's military response to the Hamas attack and ongoing security operations against militant groups in Gaza and the West Bank.
