The secret ballot results saw Germany receive only 104 votes compared to Portugal's 134 and Austria's 131—a dramatic reversal for a country that had won every previous contest since 1977.

The loss highlights growing international frustration with Western double standards in foreign policy. Germany has been vocal in condemning Russian actions in Ukraine while simultaneously defending Israeli military operations that have drawn widespread criticism. As one left-wing German politician noted, "When it comes to Russia, international law counts, but when it comes to the USA and Israel, it doesn't." This inconsistent application of principles appears to have cost Berlin dearly on the world stage.

He defended Germany's position on Israel as a "special responsibility," rejecting any suggestion of hypocrisy. The defeat represents a significant blow to Germany's international standing and raises questions about whether principle-based foreign policy has given way to political convenience.
