In what became the most expensive congressional primary in American history, Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY) was defeated Tuesday by Trump-endorsed challenger Ed Gallrein, following an unprecedented intervention by both the former president and pro-Israel lobbying organizations.

Gallrein won with 54.9% of the vote after a campaign bolstered by $15.5 million from groups including the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) and the Republican Jewish Coalition, according to Federal Election Commission filings. The total spending in the race reached nearly $33 million, underscoring the stakes for both Trump's consolidation of party control and lobbying groups seeking to remove critics from Congress.

Massie, who had held Kentucky's fourth congressional district since 2012, became a target after calling for the release of unredacted Epstein files and opposing Trump's omnibus spending bill. He has been an outspoken critic of foreign influence in American politics, particularly regarding Israeli lobbying efforts. Last week, Massie characterized the election as "a referendum on whether Israel gets to buy seats in Congress."

In his concession speech, Massie made pointed remarks about his opponent's backers. "I would have come out sooner but I had to call my opponent and concede and it took a while to find Ed Gallrein in Tel Aviv," he told supporters. The result marks another consolidation of Trump's influence over the Republican Party, following similar primary victories by Trump-backed candidates in Louisiana and other states.
