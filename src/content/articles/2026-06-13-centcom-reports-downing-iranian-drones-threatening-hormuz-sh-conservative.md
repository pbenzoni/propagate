U.S. Central Command announced Saturday that American forces successfully intercepted and destroyed multiple Iranian drones allegedly targeting commercial vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. The military operation comes as Washington and Tehran work toward finalizing an agreement to reduce months of heightened tensions in the strategically vital waterway.

According to CENTCOM's statement, Iran launched "multiple one-way attack drones in an attempt to strike commercial ships transiting the Strait of Hormuz." U.S. forces neutralized all threats "in recent hours as traffic flow through the strait continues unimpeded." The military emphasized that the corridor—which typically handles roughly a quarter of global seaborne oil and liquefied natural gas trade—remains open for transit.

Sources indicate negotiations have progressed over the past two months following escalated conflict that began in late February.
