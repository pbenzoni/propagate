Unverified videos circulating on social media appear to show a Russian strike near Kiev, with footage depicting bright objects descending rapidly from the sky followed by powerful flashes. Ukrainian media and Telegram channels claim the videos captured a Russian Oreshnik intermediate-range hypersonic missile strike on targets in Belaya Tserkov, though Moscow has not officially confirmed the launch.

The reported strike follows Russian President Vladimir Putin's direct order to the Defense Ministry to "submit proposals" for a response to a Ukrainian drone attack on a school dormitory in the Lugansk People's Republic that left 21 people dead—mostly children—and 42 injured. The dormitory, part of Starobelsk college, was hit by multiple waves of Ukrainian drones while students were sleeping inside on Friday morning.

Governor Leonid Pasechnik declared two days of mourning and called the attack "pure evil," saying those responsible must face "deserved and inevitable punishment." Moscow has characterized the strike as a deliberate "terrorist act" targeting civilians. The US Embassy in Kiev had warned American citizens of a "potentially significant air attack" within 24 hours prior to the reported strike.

Russia first publicly acknowledged use of the Oreshnik system in November 2024, when Putin confirmed it had been used against the Yuzhmash military-industrial facility. The system is designed to deliver multiple warheads at hypersonic speed and has been described by Moscow as practically impossible for current air defenses to intercept.
