The move represents a troubling expansion of how repressive governments can leverage critical infrastructure as a tool of coercion.

While Iranian state media has claimed that disrupting these cables could affect up to 70% of global internet traffic—a figure experts consider exaggerated—the threat to regional connectivity remains severe. Countries across the Arabian Peninsula would face significant communication disruptions if Iran follows through on its threats.
