French authorities have summoned Elon Musk for a voluntary interview as part of an ongoing investigation into X (formerly Twitter) over serious allegations involving child safety and platform governance. According to the Wall Street Journal, prosecutors are examining claims that the social media platform failed to prevent the spread of AI-generated child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and harmful deepfake content.

The investigation, which formally began in January 2025 following a complaint by French lawmakers, has since expanded to include multiple allegations. These include complicity in distributing pornographic images of minors, creation of non-consensual sexual deepfakes, Holocaust denial, and data privacy violations. The probe reportedly intensified after X's Grok AI assistant generated an estimated 3 million sexualized images in late 2025, including thousands that appeared to depict children and non-consensual deepfakes of public figures such as Selena Gomez, Taylor Swift, and former Vice President Kamala Harris.

French officials initially raised concerns about algorithmic changes on X that they said promoted hateful and discriminatory content. The platform has denied wrongdoing, characterizing the investigation as politically motivated censorship. However, reported incidents — including Grok generating Holocaust denial content before backtracking and calling it a "glitch" — have raised questions about content moderation and AI safety on the platform.

According to NBC News, while X implemented some restrictions on Grok's image generation capabilities in January, the AI assistant reportedly still fulfills some requests for inappropriate content in certain cases. France's attempt to coordinate with the U.S. Department of Justice on the investigation was reportedly declined, with American officials expressing concern about involvement in what they perceived as a politically charged proceeding.
