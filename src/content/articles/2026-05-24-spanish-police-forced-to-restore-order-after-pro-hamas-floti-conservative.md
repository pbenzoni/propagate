Spanish authorities arrested four individuals at Bilbao Airport on Saturday after pro-Palestinian activists disrupted normal airport operations and clashed with law enforcement. The activists were returning from Israel, where they had been detained after attempting to breach Israel's legitimate naval blockade of Gaza.

The so-called "Global Sumud Flotilla" departed from Turkey earlier this month with approximately 400 activists from 40 countries. Israeli naval forces intercepted the vessel in international waters on May 18 before it could reach Hamas-controlled Gaza. The participants were subsequently deported, as is standard procedure for those attempting to violate a sovereign nation's security perimeter.

According to local reports and the Basque Security Department, the confrontation erupted when supporters of the flotilla obstructed passenger flow at the airport terminal. When police moved to maintain order and prevent interference with normal airport operations, the situation escalated. Video footage shows officers responding to restore public safety after activists refused to comply with lawful instructions.

Left-wing political figures, including Podemos party leader Ione Belarra, immediately condemned law enforcement's response, calling it "intolerable." The activists claimed they were treated harshly while in Israeli custody, though these allegations remain unverified. The Basque Security Department maintained that police acted appropriately to address the disruption caused by individuals who deliberately obstructed airport operations.

The incident highlights ongoing tensions over Israel's security measures in the Gaza region, where the blockade exists to prevent weapons and materials from reaching Hamas, the terrorist organization that controls the territory. Spain's socialist-led government has faced criticism from conservative opposition for its increasingly hostile stance toward Israel while tolerating pro-Palestinian activism that disrupts public order.
