Paris erupted into violence Saturday night as mobs torched vehicles, attacked police officers, and destroyed property following Paris Saint-Germain's Champions League victory over Arsenal. French authorities detained 416 people as the celebration quickly devolved into lawless chaos on the streets of the nation's capital.

Approximately 20,000 people had gathered on the Champs-Elysees to watch the match, which took place in Budapest, Hungary. After PSG's 4-3 penalty shootout victory, rioters set scooters ablaze, launched fireworks at law enforcement, and vandalized multiple businesses. One police officer was injured in the violence. According to reports, six vehicles and two businesses were damaged, while a kiosk near the Champs-Elysees was set on fire and a bus stop was destroyed.

Interior Minister Laurent Nunez confirmed the arrests as police worked to restore order throughout the city. Officers detained several individuals outside the Parc des Princes stadium as they attempted to maintain control of the deteriorating situation.

This marks the second consecutive year that PSG's Champions League victory has triggered destructive riots in Paris. Last year's celebration resulted in two deaths and nearly 500 arrests, raising serious questions about public safety and the ability of French authorities to maintain law and order during large gatherings.
