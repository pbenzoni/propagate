The upcoming London meeting, organized by the British think tank Chatham House, will address the "evolving security situation in Europe and beyond" and include NATO cyber and innovation technology officials. According to internal communications from the Writers' Guild of Great Britain, the conversations have already "inspired" at least three separate projects.

The meetings come amid significant internal tensions within the alliance. President Trump has repeatedly criticized NATO as a "paper tiger," particularly after multiple member states declined to join recent military actions.
