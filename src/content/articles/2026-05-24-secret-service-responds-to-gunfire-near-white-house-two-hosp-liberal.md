The White House grounds were placed under temporary lockdown Saturday evening after gunshots were heard near the complex, prompting Secret Service officers to clear the North Lawn and direct journalists to shelter in the press briefing room.

ABC News correspondent Selina Wang reported hearing what sounded like "dozens of gunshots" while recording video on the North Lawn. Secret Service personnel, with weapons drawn, instructed those present to immediately move indoors. According to NBC News correspondent Julie Tsirkin, approximately 20 to 30 shots were heard outside the White House perimeter.

Two individuals were transported to area hospitals following the incident—a suspected gunman listed in critical condition and a possible bystander in serious condition. No Secret Service agents were reported injured. CBS News correspondent Jennifer Jacobs indicated that Secret Service personnel may have returned fire near the intersection of 17th Street and Pennsylvania Avenue NW.

President Donald Trump was inside the White House during the incident. The lockdown was lifted at approximately 6:45 p.m. local time. FBI Director Kash Patel confirmed that federal agents were providing support to the Secret Service response.

The incident follows another security event earlier this month when law enforcement shot a man near the Washington Monument after he opened fire on officers, highlighting ongoing security challenges in the nation's capital.
