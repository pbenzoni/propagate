The move follows a brief opening of the strategic waterway during a ten-day ceasefire between Israel and Lebanon.

Tehran initially declared the strait fully open to commercial vessels on Friday, leading to a temporary drop in oil prices. However, Trump quickly countered that the U.S. blockade of Iranian ports "will remain in full force" until a peace agreement is reached. According to media reports, the Trump administration has demanded Iran surrender its enriched uranium stockpile—a demand Iranian officials have firmly rejected, calling the stockpile "as sacred as Iranian soil."

The Iranian Foreign Ministry maintains that Tehran has the right to restrict passage through the strait due to threats to national security. Multiple incidents have been reported in the region, including alleged gunboat fire on a tanker, though no injuries were confirmed.
