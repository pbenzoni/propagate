The escalation comes as reports indicate President Trump is prepared to maintain the naval blockade of Iranian ports "for months" in an attempt to extract more favorable terms from Tehran. Iranian officials have responded by blocking vessels from the U.S. and its allies from transiting the Strait of Hormuz and warning of potential military action to break the blockade.
