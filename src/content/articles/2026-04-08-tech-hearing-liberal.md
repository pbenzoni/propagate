Another platform executive sat in another hearing room this week and read another opening statement about how much the company cares. The senators took turns. Some were furious on brand and some were furious for the cameras and a couple of them asked questions that were clearly written by staff who had actually read the filings. Nothing will happen because nothing is designed to happen.

There is a version of this conversation that is about power — about who decides what speech is amplified, about who carries the externalities of a feed optimized for engagement, about whose labor moderates the worst of it and under what conditions. That conversation requires rules. Rules require a legislature that can write them. We do not have that legislature. We have this hearing room.

The test of a democracy is whether its institutions can actually govern the technologies their citizens depend on. On the current evidence, the answer is not encouraging. But the work of demanding better — louder, in more places, with clearer asks — is still worth doing. Nothing else has ever worked.
