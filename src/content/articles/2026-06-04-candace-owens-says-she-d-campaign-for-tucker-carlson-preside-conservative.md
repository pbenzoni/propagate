Conservative commentator Candace Owens made clear she would throw her full support behind Tucker Carlson if the popular journalist decided to run for president, while dismissing any personal ambitions for the White House.

Speaking with RT at the St. Petersburg International Economic Forum, Owens joked that she would only seek the presidency herself under one condition: "I'd only run for dictator. I'm not dealing with Congress, I'm not dealing with lobbying, I'm not dealing with Lindsey Graham calling for another war." Her quip highlighted frustration with the gridlock and special interests that plague Washington.

The former Daily Wire host said she has no near-term interest in political office because of what she called the "inauthenticity" of the nation's capital. However, Owens emphasized she would be ready to campaign across the country for Carlson if he chose to run, comparing such an effort to her previous political work alongside Charlie Kirk.

Owens rose to national prominence in the late 2010s by encouraging black voters to reconsider their traditional support for Democrats. She became one of the most recognized voices in conservative media before parting ways with the Daily Wire following disagreements over commentary on Israel's war in Gaza. She remains an influential figure among grassroots conservatives who value her willingness to challenge established narratives.
