Two German military personnel were injured in an attack outside NATO's operational headquarters in Brunssum, Netherlands, according to reports from German news outlets. The incident occurred Monday evening when the servicemembers, who were not in uniform, were assaulted in a wooded area near the base.

According to a spokesperson for the German Defense Ministry, both individuals sustained minor injuries and are in stable condition. The ministry characterized the incident as "security-related." Reports indicate the attackers wore masks and shouted anti-NATO slogans during the assault, though their identities and complete motivations remain under investigation by Dutch military police.

One of the officers reported that a group of approximately ten individuals initially demanded money before he fled to a nearby hotel and contacted authorities. By the time police arrived, the group had allegedly attacked the second officer before dispersing. No arrests have been made.

The attack comes at a time of elevated security concerns across Europe. NATO raised its alert level following escalating regional tensions, with specific guidance issued to military personnel about appearing in uniform outside secure facilities.
