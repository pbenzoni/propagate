A team of Thai police officers successfully arrested a suspected drug dealer during a street festival in Lop Buri Province last week, using an unconventional undercover approach that has since attracted international attention. Officers dressed as performers in a dance troupe to approach their target without detection during the community celebration.

According to Tha Luang Police, the operation resulted in the arrest of a suspect on drug and illegal gambling charges. Authorities reportedly seized methamphetamine pills, materials suspected to be drug-distribution supplies, and a mobile phone allegedly connected to illegal online gambling operations.
