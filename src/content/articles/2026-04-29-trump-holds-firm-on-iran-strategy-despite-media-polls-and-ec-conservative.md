The administration views the strategy as necessary to counter Tehran's nuclear ambitions, even as mainstream media outlets highlight declining poll numbers and rising gas prices.

According to unnamed officials, the president sees the blockade as a middle path between full military escalation and what some defense hawks warn would be a dangerous retreat that emboldens Iranian aggression. The approach aims to force Tehran to abandon its nuclear program and comply with longstanding American demands regarding its enriched uranium stockpile. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has emphasized that any settlement must address the nuclear issue as a core component, rejecting Iranian proposals that would defer those critical discussions.
