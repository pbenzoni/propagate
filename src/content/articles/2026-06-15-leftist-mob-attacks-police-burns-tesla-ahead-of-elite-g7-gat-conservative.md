Thousands of left-wing activists descended on Geneva this weekend, turning what organizers called a peaceful protest into violent chaos ahead of the G7 summit scheduled to begin Monday at a nearby French resort town. Riot police were forced to deploy water cannons and tear gas after demonstrators hurled projectiles at officers.

The unrest drew a coalition of approximately 60 organizations spanning feminist groups, trade unions, and pro-Palestinian activists—a familiar roster of progressive causes. While organizers claimed 20,000 attendees, police estimated the actual crowd at around 7,000. The violence included smashing bank windows and destroying a Tesla vehicle, which was burned amid the turmoil. Tesla CEO Elon Musk recently became the world's first trillionaire following a successful $75 billion SpaceX IPO.

The Swiss government had already closed 25 of 35 border crossings with France due to security concerns surrounding the summit. Demonstrators carried banners depicting President Donald Trump as a dinosaur and chanted slogans against capitalism and what they termed "imperialism"—revealing the ideological motivations behind the so-called protest.

The G7 summit in Evian-les-Bains comes as Ukrainian leader Vladimir Zelensky is expected to attend, seeking an additional $20 billion from NATO countries to fund Kiev's ongoing conflict with Russia.
