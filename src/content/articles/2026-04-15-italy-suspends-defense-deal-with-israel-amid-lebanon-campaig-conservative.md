Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni announced her government will suspend the automatic renewal of a longstanding defense cooperation agreement with Israel, citing concerns over the current military situation in Lebanon. The decision marks a significant shift for Italy's conservative government, previously considered among Israel's strongest European allies.

The agreement, originally ratified in 2005 and renewed every five years, covers defense industry cooperation, military equipment trade, technical exchanges, and personnel training. Meloni stated the suspension came "in light of the current situation," adding that "when there are things we don't agree with, we act accordingly."

The move follows multiple friction points between Rome and Jerusalem, including an incident where Israeli forces fired warning shots at an Italian peacekeeping convoy outside Beirut. Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani summoned Israel's ambassador over what Meloni called a "completely unacceptable" incident.
