The machines were demonstrated by the National Chung-Shan Institute of Science and Technology at an event in Taipei this week.

The 52-kilogram robots—based on technology from U.S. firm Ghost Robotics—can navigate difficult terrain and operate in all weather conditions. Institute officials say Taiwan's military has expressed urgent interest in deploying the machines for coastal surveillance, maritime patrols, and perimeter security at military installations. The development comes as Taipei recently approved approximately $280 million in defense spending for U.S. arms purchases.

Chinese President Xi Jinping has repeatedly stated that while China seeks peaceful reunification, it reserves the right to use force.
