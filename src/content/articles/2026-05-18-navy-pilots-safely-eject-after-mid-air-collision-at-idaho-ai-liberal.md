Two US Navy EA-18G Growler aircraft collided during a demonstration flight at the Gunfighter Skies Air Show at Mountain Home Air Force Base in Idaho, prompting all four crew members to eject safely. The incident occurred while the jets were performing in close formation at low altitude.

Video footage captured the harrowing moment when the two electronic warfare aircraft appeared to become entangled mid-flight before spiraling toward the ground. Within seconds of the collision, all four pilots successfully ejected, with their brightly colored parachutes visible against the sky. "All four members of the aircrew successfully ejected and are being evaluated by medical personnel," said Naval Air Forces spokeswoman Commander Amelia Umayam.

The EA-18G Growler is a specialized Boeing aircraft designed for electronic warfare missions and based on the F/A-18F Super Hornet platform. Introduced in the late 2000s, these carrier-based jets replaced the earlier EA-6B Prowler in conducting critical electronic warfare operations. The cause of the collision remains under investigation.
