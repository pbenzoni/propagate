A drone struck a residential apartment building in Galati, Romania on Friday, injuring two people and forcing the evacuation of approximately 70 residents, according to emergency services. The unmanned aerial vehicle hit the 10th floor of the building, sparking a fire that required response from firefighters, police, and bomb disposal units.

Catalin Sion, a spokesman for Galati emergency services, confirmed that two individuals were hospitalized with minor injuries, while two others received treatment for panic attacks following the incident. Romania's Defense Ministry released a statement claiming the drone originated from Russia and was involved in strikes targeting Ukraine, highlighting the growing risks of conflict spillover for neighboring NATO member states.

This incident marks the latest in a series of reported airspace violations in the region. In April, Romanian officials said a UAV damaged a house and electrical infrastructure near the same city. Russia has denied responsibility for previous incidents, stating there is no definitive proof the drones were Russian in origin.
