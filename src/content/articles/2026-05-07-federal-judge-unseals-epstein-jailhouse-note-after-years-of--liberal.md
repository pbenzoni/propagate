A federal judge has ordered the unsealing of a handwritten note attributed to Jeffrey Epstein, the wealthy financier whose death in federal custody became a flashpoint for questions about power, privilege, and accountability in the American justice system.

The note, reportedly discovered by Epstein's cellmate Nicholas Tartaglione after a suicide attempt in July 2019, had been kept under seal in court files for nearly seven years. "They investigated me for months – found nothing!!! So 15-year-old charges resurface," the barely legible message reads, according to reports. The document was released following a transparency petition by the *New York Times*, with prosecutors supporting public disclosure.

Epstein died in his Manhattan jail cell in August 2019 while awaiting trial on sex-trafficking charges. His death was officially ruled a suicide, though it continues to fuel public skepticism given his extensive connections to wealthy and politically powerful figures. While his associate Ghislaine Maxwell was convicted of sex-trafficking offenses, no other high-profile individuals in Epstein's network have faced criminal charges related to the case.

The release of this document underscores ongoing concerns about transparency and justice for survivors.
