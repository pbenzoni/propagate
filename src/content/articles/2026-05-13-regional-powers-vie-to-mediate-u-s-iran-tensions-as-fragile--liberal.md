According to reports, Tehran has expressed skepticism about the sincerity of Washington's engagement, pointing to previous instances where diplomatic overtures preceded escalation rather than de-escalation. Iranian officials have publicly questioned whether statements about negotiations serve genuine diplomatic purposes or function primarily as political messaging aimed at domestic and international audiences.
