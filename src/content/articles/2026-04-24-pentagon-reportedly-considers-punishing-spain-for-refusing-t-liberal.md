The document, which reportedly circulated at high levels within the Pentagon, expresses frustration with European NATO members who denied the U.S. access to their military bases and airspace during the conflict that began in late February, according to an American official familiar with its contents. However, NATO officials have emphasized that the alliance's charter contains no provisions for suspending or expelling member states. The only exit mechanism is voluntary withdrawal under Article 13, which requires one year's formal notice. Responding to the Reuters report, Sánchez dismissed the threat, stating he was "absolutely not worried" and affirming that Spain remains "a reliable member within NATO."
