The suspect, identified as Cole Allen, sent a 1,052-word note to relatives approximately ten minutes before the attack at the Washington Hilton. In the document, Allen described himself as a "half-black, half-white" American citizen and framed his actions as holding government officials accountable. He explicitly named Trump administration officials as targets while excluding FBI Director Kash Patel, and stated he prioritized targets "from highest-ranking to lowest."

Allen's manifesto referenced multiple concerns including conditions in immigration detention facilities, U.S. military strikes abroad, and the Jeffrey Epstein case. "I'm not the person raped in a detention camp. I'm not the fisherman executed without trial.
