Israeli Defense Forces launched extensive military operations across southern Lebanon Wednesday after designating the region south of the Zahrani River a combat zone. The strikes targeted what Israel identified as Hezbollah command centers, weapons depots, and launch sites used by the Iran-backed terrorist organization.

The IDF issued evacuation warnings to Lebanese civilians in the Tyre region before commencing operations, with military spokesman Avichay Adraee urging residents to move to safer areas. According to Israeli military sources, approximately 550 Hezbollah-linked targets have been struck since the beginning of the week, with operations around Tyre continuing.

Hezbollah has claimed its fighters are conducting what it calls "retaliatory operations" against Israeli forces in southern Lebanon, asserting these actions respond to alleged Israeli ceasefire violations. The escalation occurs as indirect talks between the United States and Iran continue, with Tehran demanding Israel halt military operations against Hezbollah as a condition for extending any ceasefire with Washington.

President Trump has previously stated that Israel should limit itself to "surgical" strikes in Lebanon. Lebanon's Health Ministry reports that over 3,200 people have been killed since Israel's military operation against Hezbollah began following a US-Israeli action against Iran in late February. The Lebanese army confirmed one soldier was killed near a military position in the Bekaa region during the strikes.
