Progressive political commentators Hasan Piker and Cenk Uygur have been barred from entering the United Kingdom after criticizing Israel's military operations in Gaza, raising concerns about free speech and the influence of foreign lobbying on democratic nations.

Piker, a popular Twitch streamer with millions of followers, was scheduled to speak at the SXSW London festival when he learned his visa had been revoked. "The West is betraying 'liberal values' for a genocidal fascist foreign government," he wrote on social media, suggesting the decision came under pressure from pro-Israel advocates. Both commentators have been outspoken critics of Israel's war in Gaza, which has killed tens of thousands of Palestinians and displaced most of the territory's population. Pro-Israel organizations, including the Anti-Defamation League, have accused Piker of anti-Semitism, pointing to controversial statements he made on podcasts. UK Labour MP David Taylor argued that Piker's presence would not serve "the public good."
