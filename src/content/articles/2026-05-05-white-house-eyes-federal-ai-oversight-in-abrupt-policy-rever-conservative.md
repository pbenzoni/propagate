The Trump administration is weighing a significant shift in artificial intelligence policy that could subject new AI models to federal oversight before they reach the market, according to a New York Times report citing administration sources.

The White House is reportedly exploring the creation of an AI working group that would bring together government officials and technology executives to develop oversight mechanisms, potentially including a formal review process for advanced AI systems. While officials frame the initiative as a safeguard against potential cyberattacks, sources indicate it would also grant federal agencies early access to cutting-edge models to evaluate their utility for Pentagon operations.

The proposed framework could mirror the United Kingdom's AI Security Institute, which assesses advanced models for national security risks. Major American AI companies already participate in voluntary testing through the federal Center for AI Standards and Innovation, though that program maintains a lighter regulatory touch focused on building industry trust rather than imposing restrictions.

The reported policy consideration represents a marked departure from President Trump's previous stance championing minimal regulation of artificial intelligence. Since his return to office, Trump has consistently warned against allowing "politics or stupid rules" to hamper American AI development, calling the technology essential to maintaining competitive advantage over China. Any new oversight mechanism would likely be implemented through executive order, though no timeline has been established.

The shift in approach gained momentum following Anthropic's decision to withhold its Claude Mythos model from public release over concerns about software vulnerability exposure. Tensions escalated further when the Pentagon labeled Anthropic a "supply-chain risk" after the company refused to adjust safeguards related to surveillance and autonomous weapons systems—a designation Anthropic is now contesting in federal court.
