Search-and-rescue operations continued through Saturday, reportedly hampered by additional Ukrainian drone attacks on the area.

Russian President Vladimir Putin called the strike a "terrorist attack" and ordered military preparations for retaliation. Russia also requested an emergency UN Security Council session to address the incident. Ukraine's envoy called the Security Council session "a shameless attempt" to manipulate public opinion and dismissed the evidence as "pure propaganda."
