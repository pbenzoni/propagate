The exchange came after Merz questioned U.S. handling of the Iran conflict, claiming Washington was being "humiliated" and lacked a clear plan.

In a Truth Social post Thursday, Trump didn't mince words: the German chancellor "should spend more time on ending the war with Russia/Ukraine (Where he has been totally ineffective!), and fixing his broken Country, especially Immigration and Energy, and less time on interfering with those that are getting rid of the Iran Nuclear threat."

Merz has rejected German participation in the Iran conflict, calling it "not a NATO matter."

Trump also took aim at Merz's stance on Iran's nuclear ambitions, asserting the German leader "thinks it's OK for Iran to have a Nuclear Weapon" and "doesn't know what he's talking about." The rebuke underscores the President's broader frustration with European NATO members who have declined to assist in reopening the Strait of Hormuz, a critical shipping corridor threatened by Iranian aggression.
