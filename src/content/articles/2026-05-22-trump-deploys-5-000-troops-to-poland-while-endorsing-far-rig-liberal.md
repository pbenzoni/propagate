The announcement comes amid growing confusion over U.S. military commitments in Europe. Last week, the Pentagon abruptly canceled a planned rotation of 4,000 troops into Poland, alarming Polish officials before Vice President J.D. Vance attempted damage control by characterizing it as merely a "delay."
