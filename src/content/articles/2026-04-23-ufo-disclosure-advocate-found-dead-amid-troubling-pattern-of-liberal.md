David Wilcock, a 53-year-old UFO researcher and author with hundreds of thousands of social media followers, has died in what authorities have ruled a suicide. The Boulder County Sheriff's Office reported that deputies responding to a mental health crisis call on Monday encountered an armed man at a residence northeast of Nederland, Colorado, who died by suicide shortly after their arrival.

Wilcock was a prominent voice in the UFO disclosure movement, which advocates for government transparency regarding unidentified aerial phenomena and potential extraterrestrial contact. He authored several books and maintained a substantial online following devoted to discussions of paranormal phenomena and calls for declassification of government records.

His death occurs against a backdrop of growing concern about a series of unexplained deaths and disappearances involving at least ten U.S. scientists and researchers since 2023, many of whom held security clearances for sensitive nuclear and space programs. President Trump recently announced that he has directed authorities to investigate what he characterized as "pretty serious stuff," though the nature of any connection between these cases remains unclear.

Two days before his death, Wilcock posted on social media that he had "had some very intense stuff going on this weekend," adding "I want you all to know how much I love and appreciate you." The Boulder County Coroner's Office formally identified Wilcock on Wednesday. No other individuals were found at the residence during the subsequent search.
