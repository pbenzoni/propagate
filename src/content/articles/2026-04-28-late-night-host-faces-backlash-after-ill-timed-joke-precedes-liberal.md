Two days later, security personnel intercepted an armed individual attempting to enter the correspondents' dinner, forcing the evacuation of attendees including the president and First Lady. "We all have the right to free speech," Kimmel said, while expressing sympathy for those who experienced the "scary" and "traumatic" security incident.
