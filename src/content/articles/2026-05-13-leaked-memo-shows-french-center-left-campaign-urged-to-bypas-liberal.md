A leaked internal campaign memo has revealed troubling advice given to French presidential hopeful Raphaël Glucksmann: prioritize wealthy, older voters while sidelining working-class families, single parents, and younger citizens ahead of the 2027 election, according to Politico.

The confidential document identified Glucksmann's strongest potential base as moderate, pro-European left-wing voters over 50, including retirees and upper-middle-class professionals. The memo specifically recommended *against* targeting lower-income groups and residents of immigrant-heavy neighborhoods—communities seen as more likely to support Jean-Luc Mélenchon's progressive La France Insoumise movement.

Glucksmann, a Member of European Parliament and co-founder of the center-left Place Publique party allied with the Socialist Party, has not formally declared his candidacy. His team attempted to distance themselves from the memo, calling it merely a "working document" rather than official strategy. Critics have long described the 46-year-old politician as disconnected from working-class struggles, pointing to his affluent background and elite professional circles.

The leaked strategy stands in stark contrast to progressive values of economic justice and broad-based coalition building. With President Emmanuel Macron constitutionally barred from seeking another term, the 2027 race remains wide open—though polls currently show far-right National Rally leader Jordan Bardella leading the field. The memo raises urgent questions about whether centrist and center-left parties are abandoning the very constituencies they claim to represent.
