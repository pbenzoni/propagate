Israel has approved construction of a new military complex on the site of a former United Nations Relief and Works Agency (UNRWA) headquarters in East Jerusalem, a move Defense Minister Israel Katz described as representing "sovereignty, Zionism and security" for the nation.

The Israel Defense Forces seized and partially demolished the UNRWA compound in 2024 after Israeli lawmakers passed legislation banning the organization's operations in areas under Israeli control. The action followed accusations that UNRWA employees had colluded with Hamas during the October 7, 2023 terrorist attack on Israeli communities. Following an internal investigation, UNRWA itself acknowledged that available evidence indicated nine of its employees "may have been involved in the attacks of October 7."

According to Israeli media, the new nine-acre compound near Ammunition Hill will house an IDF museum, recruitment offices, and facilities for the defense minister. In December, the Defense Ministry signed an agreement with Jerusalem's municipal government to establish the new defense headquarters and relocate several military colleges to the site.

Katz stated there was "nothing more symbolic or just" than establishing defense institutions on the former UNRWA property, which was originally created to support Palestinians displaced during the 1948 Arab-Israeli war. The move represents Israel's determination to maintain control over its capital city and protect its citizens from terrorist threats, even as international organizations and UN agencies continue to criticize Israeli military operations in Gaza.
