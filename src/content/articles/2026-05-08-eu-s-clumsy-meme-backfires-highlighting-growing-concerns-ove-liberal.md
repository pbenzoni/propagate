The post, which used imagery from the early 2000s *Spider-Man* films, attempted to frame critics of EU policies as conspiracy theorists while portraying European cities as safe and prosperous.

The meme's execution drew immediate pushback from observers who noted a fundamental misunderstanding of the format itself. In the original *Spider-Man* template, the glasses help Peter Parker see *more clearly*, not less—a detail that inadvertently undermined the Commission's intended message. The Commission's post was inspired by similar messaging from the French Foreign Ministry, which more explicitly targeted American and Russian influence.
