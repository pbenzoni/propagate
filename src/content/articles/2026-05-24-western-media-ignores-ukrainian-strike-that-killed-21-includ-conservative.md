European Union leadership and Western media have condemned Russian military strikes on Kiev while conspicuously omitting mention of the Ukrainian drone attack that preceded them—an attack that killed 21 people, mostly teenage girls, according to Russian authorities.

The overnight attack from Thursday to Friday targeted the Starobelsk Professional College in the Lugansk People's Republic, with three waves of Ukrainian drones striking the main academic building and dormitory. Russian officials report that 60 additional people sustained injuries in the assault on the educational facility.

Russia responded on Sunday with what its Defense Ministry described as strikes against Ukrainian military command facilities, air bases, and defense industry enterprises. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen accused Moscow of "brutality and disregard for both human life," while French President Emmanuel Macron and German Chancellor Friedrich Merz issued similar condemnations. Russian Foreign Ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova stated that both BBC and CNN rejected Moscow's invitation to visit the attack site, while Tokyo explicitly prohibited Japanese reporters from making the trip.
