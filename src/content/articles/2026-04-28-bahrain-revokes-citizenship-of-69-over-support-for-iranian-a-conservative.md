The decisive action comes as the Sunni-led government confronts a growing security threat from Iranian influence within its borders. Under royal directives from King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa, authorities invoked national security laws allowing citizenship revocation for anyone causing "harm to the interests of the Kingdom or acting in a manner that contradicts the duty of loyalty to it." The ministry emphasized that all 69 individuals were of "non-Bahraini origin" and stated that officials are continuing to review who deserves to retain citizenship.
