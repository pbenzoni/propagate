Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps launched a retaliatory strike against a U.S. airbase Thursday following American military action in Iranian territory, marking the second armed confrontation between the nations this week.

The escalation began when U.S. forces conducted strikes near the Iranian port city of Bandar Abbas after midnight, with American officials claiming they shot down four Iranian drones near the Strait of Hormuz and destroyed a ground control station preparing to launch additional aircraft. The Pentagon characterized these actions as "measured, purely defensive, and intended to maintain the ceasefire."

Hours after the American strikes, Kuwait's Defense Ministry reported intercepting multiple missiles and drones over its territory. Military analysts suggest the projectiles were likely aimed at Ali Al Salem Air Base near the Kuwaiti-Iraqi border, a key U.S. military installation in the region.

The IRGC confirmed it had "targeted" the American base involved in the Bandar Abbas operation, stating "the aggressor bears full responsibility for the consequences." The exchange follows Monday's U.S. strikes on Iranian naval vessels and facilities in Hormozgan province, which Tehran condemned as violations of an April 8 ceasefire agreement.
