Telegram founder Pavel Durov is sounding the alarm about European government overreach, accusing French authorities of massive hypocrisy in their pursuit of Elon Musk's X platform. The tech entrepreneur claims France is illegally harvesting citizen data while simultaneously prosecuting social media companies for the very same alleged violations.

Durov's warning comes as Paris prosecutors expand their criminal investigation into X, adding new charges related to AI-generated content and data practices. "The French government is accusing X of the very things the French government itself is doing," Durov stated, calling on the international community to defend the platform against what he describes as an "immoral assault" by the French state.

The Telegram co-founder believes the timing reveals deeper motivations: "The French government is panicking. Durov has personal experience with French legal targeting—he was arrested at a Paris airport in 2024 on charges related to content on his platform, though he has since been allowed to leave the country.
