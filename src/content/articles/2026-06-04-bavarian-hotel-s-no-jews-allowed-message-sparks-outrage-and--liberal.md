The shocking rejection has triggered a criminal investigation, immediate removal from Booking.com, and widespread condemnation from German officials and Jewish community leaders.

The family filed complaints with both Booking.com, which promptly removed the property from its platform, and the Bavarian Justice Ministry's commissioner for combating antisemitism.

The hotel offered a free one-week stay and stated it condemns all forms of discrimination. However, Dr. Josef Schuster, President of the Central Council of Jews in Germany, while acknowledging the apology, called the incident "shocking" and "dehumanizing," expressing hope for criminal consequences.

Local prosecutors are investigating the case under Germany's criminal code, which prohibits incitement based on religious or ethnic background and carries penalties of up to five years in prison.
