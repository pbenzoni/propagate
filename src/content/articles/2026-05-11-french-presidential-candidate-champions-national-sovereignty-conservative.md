Florian Philippot, leader of the Patriots party, announced his 2027 candidacy with a platform centered on reclaiming French sovereignty from supranational institutions.

Philippot told RT that he wants France to "regain its independence by leaving all the supranational globalist structures: the EU, the euro, NATO." The former National Front vice president criticized what he sees as a destructive policy of hostility toward Russia, advocating instead for "dialogue and friendship" rather than the current approach of "mistrust, war, and insults."

The candidate's platform includes ending EU-imposed sanctions to restore the flow of affordable Russian gas and oil to French consumers. He also called for withdrawing from free trade agreements like Mercosur, which he argues are decimating French farmers, and for France to take back control of its borders and immigration policy. Philippot advocates abandoning the euro in favor of a national currency better suited to France's economy, arguing that EU membership has weakened the country's industrial base.

Philippot joins a crowded field of approximately 30 potential candidates in France's 2027 presidential race. His sovereignty-first platform represents a growing sentiment among voters frustrated with decisions made in Brussels, Berlin, and Washington rather than Paris.
