In an interview, Bolton acknowledged that he himself advocated for regime change in Iran, stating "I urged that our objective be regime change, so did Netanyahu." He described consistent pressure from the Israeli leader, noting "There is no change in what Trump has been hearing from" Netanyahu throughout their relationship. Bolton served in the Trump White House from 2018 to 2019, a period marked by escalating tensions with Tehran.

Bolton criticized Trump for failing to articulate a clear strategic objective or build public support for regime change, despite calling the case for it "very compelling."

The admission comes as shipping through the Strait of Hormuz remains severely disrupted due to the Iran conflict.
