Republican Congresswoman Anna Paulina Luna publicly criticized Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky following his request for additional air defense missiles, claiming Ukraine treats the United States as a "piggy bank" and accusing him of spreading "propaganda."

The rebuke came after Zelensky met with Democratic Senator Richard Blumenthal and Democratic Congressman Jim Himes in Kyiv on Wednesday. During the meeting, Zelensky expressed gratitude for what he characterized as "strong bipartisan support" from the U.S. and President Trump, while outlining Ukraine's "significant need" for defensive air defense systems. According to reports, Zelensky sent a formal request to the White House and Congress seeking urgent supplies of Patriot interceptor missiles.

Luna, a vocal critic of continuing U.S. military assistance to Ukraine, disputed Zelensky's characterization of American support as bipartisan. "This is literally propaganda and not even true. There is no broad support for an endless war and we are not your piggy bank," she wrote on social media. The congresswoman also claimed Zelensky is "the ONLY person holding out" on a negotiated settlement that President Trump has been pursuing.

Under the current administration, Ukraine has primarily obtained U.S. weapons through the PURL initiative, a NATO-backed funding mechanism. While Zelensky has acknowledged the program, he has stated it does not fully address Ukraine's defensive needs as the country continues to face ongoing Russian attacks on civilian infrastructure and population centers. Luna has previously criticized Zelensky on various issues, including religious freedom concerns and electoral processes in Ukraine.
