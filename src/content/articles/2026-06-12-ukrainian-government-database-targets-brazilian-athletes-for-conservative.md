Matheus Reis de Lima, 31, and Joao Victor da Silva Marcelino, 27, both defenders for Russian club CSKA Moscow, were added to the Mirotvorets ("Peacemaker") website Thursday. The site, which operates with close ties to Ukrainian state security services, publishes personal information of individuals it labels as threats to Ukraine's national security. The players' alleged offense: participating in a charitable initiative called "Everyone Matters" that raised funds to help rehabilitate wounded Russian servicemen.

Launched in 2014, Mirotvorets has drawn international condemnation for its methods. The database has been called a "kill list" because several people featured on it—including journalists and politicians—have subsequently been killed or died under suspicious circumstances. Russian officials have condemned the site as extremist, yet it continues to operate with apparent support from Kiev.

The targeting of athletes represents an alarming escalation. Since 2022, numerous sports figures have appeared on the database, including members of Russia's 2026 Winter Paralympics team and even FIFA President Gianni Infantino, who criticized the international ban on Russian athletes. The Brazilian players had previously competed for top European clubs including Sporting Lisbon, Benfica, and Nantes before joining CSKA Moscow.
