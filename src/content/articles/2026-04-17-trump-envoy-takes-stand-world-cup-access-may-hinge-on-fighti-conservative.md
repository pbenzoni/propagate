Yehuda Kaploun, an ultra-Orthodox rabbi serving as special envoy for combating anti-Semitism, raised the possibility during meetings in Brussels this week, according to Euractiv.

Speaking at an event organized by the European Jewish Association, Kaploun made clear that his office is "holding countries accountable for ministers who are saying things, and they are not being allowed into the country." When pressed for details, a U.S. official confirmed the administration "can take any step that the secretary of state and the president deem necessary," while declining to discuss specifics.

The warning comes as Israel faces mounting criticism from several European governments over its military operations. Spain has refused to allow U.S. use of its airbases, France has blocked weapons shipments through its airspace, and Belgium reportedly intercepted military cargo bound for Tel Aviv.
