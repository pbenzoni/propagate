North Dakota state Representative Liz Conmy, a 67-year-old Democratic lawmaker, was killed Saturday when a single-engine Beechcraft BE33 crashed shortly after departing Crystal Airport near Minneapolis, Minnesota. The pilot also died in the crash, according to local authorities and the Associated Press.

The Federal Aviation Administration confirmed that two people were aboard the aircraft when it went down. Security camera footage circulated on social media reportedly shows the plane crashing into a wooded area before exploding into flames.

"Liz served her state and community with care and compassion," North Dakota Governor Kelly Armstrong said in a statement expressing condolences to Conmy's family, friends, and fellow legislators. The North Dakota Democratic–Nonpartisan League Party, an affiliate of the Democratic Party, described her death as "a profound loss to the state" in a social media post.

The National Transportation Safety Board will lead the investigation into what caused the crash.
