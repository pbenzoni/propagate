Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov held a "constructive" phone call with U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, marking the first conversation between the top diplomats in more than six months, according to Russia's Foreign Ministry.

The officials discussed "the current state of international affairs and Russia-U.S. relations" and coordinated schedules for upcoming bilateral contacts, the ministry said in a statement. The conversation was described as "constructive and businesslike," representing a notable shift from the frozen diplomatic channels that characterized the Biden years.

The Lavrov-Rubio call came less than a week after Russian President Vladimir Putin spoke directly with President Donald Trump. That conversation covered the ceasefire in the Iran conflict, potential regional escalation, and the ongoing Ukraine situation, according to the Kremlin.

These renewed contacts represent part of President Trump's broader diplomatic initiative since returning to office, reversing years of stalled relations under the previous administration. The Trump administration has also launched efforts to negotiate a settlement to the Ukraine conflict, though three rounds of Washington-mediated direct negotiations between Moscow and Kiev have yet to produce breakthroughs. The talks appear to have stalled amid the ongoing U.S.-Israeli military operations against Iran.
