The remains of Los Alamos National Laboratory employee Melissa Casias have been found in Carson National Forest, New Mexico State Police confirmed. A handgun was discovered near the remains, though authorities have not yet determined the cause or manner of death.

Casias, who worked as an administrative assistant at the critical nuclear research facility, was reported missing last June after failing to show up for work. Her purse, identification, and cell phones were all left behind when she disappeared after visiting her daughter. The case is part of a deeply troubling pattern that has caught the attention of House oversight investigators.

Nearly a dozen individuals connected to America's most sensitive national security research have died or vanished under puzzling circumstances in recent years. The list includes another Los Alamos employee, a contractor at a facility producing components for nuclear weapons, multiple NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory personnel, a retired Air Force Major General, an MIT physicist shot dead at his home, and a Caltech astrophysicist gunned down on his porch.

President Trump called the situation "pretty serious stuff," and the House Oversight Committee has demanded briefings from the Department of Energy, the Pentagon, the FBI, and NASA. While authorities claim no confirmed link has been established among the cases, the sheer number of incidents involving personnel with access to nuclear secrets and advanced rocket technology has raised serious questions about whether America's most vital national security assets are being systematically targeted.
