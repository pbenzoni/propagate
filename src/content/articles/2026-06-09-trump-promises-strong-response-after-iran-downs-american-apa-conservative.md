President Donald Trump has vowed the United States will respond decisively after Iran downed an American AH-64 Apache attack helicopter patrolling the strategic Strait of Hormuz. The incident occurred overnight as the aircraft conducted routine operations off the coast of Oman.

US Central Command confirmed the helicopter "went down" while patrolling regional waters, with both pilots rescued approximately two hours after the incident. While the military stated the cause remains under investigation, President Trump directly attributed the attack to Iran and declared America must respond.

"There were two pilots involved, both are safe and uninjured. Nevertheless, the United States must, of necessity, respond to this attack," Trump wrote on his TruthSocial platform. The president's firm stance comes amid ongoing tensions in the region, where Iran has repeatedly challenged American military operations.

According to media reports citing unnamed US officials, the helicopter may have collided with an Iranian drone in mid-air, though investigators have not yet determined whether the incident was intentional. Iran has maintained silence on the matter despite its typical practice of publicizing such confrontations with US forces. The rescue operation notably involved maritime drones for the first time, according to CENTCOM spokesman Tim Hawkins.
