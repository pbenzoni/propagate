The confrontation at the University of Georgia event highlights emerging tensions within the conservative movement over U.S. Middle East policy. Less than 15 minutes into his appearance, Vance was repeatedly interrupted by hecklers demanding answers about civilian casualties resulting from the ongoing conflict.

In his response, Vance acknowledged the discontent among young voters, stating, "I recognize that a lot of young voters don't love the policy that we have in the Middle East."
