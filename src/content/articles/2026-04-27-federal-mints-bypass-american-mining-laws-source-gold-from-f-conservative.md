According to the investigation, the US Mint—which claims its coins connect Americans "to the founding principles of our nation"—has been sourcing gold through a Texas-based contractor, Dillon Gage, that mixes foreign gold with domestic supplies. Federal law explicitly requires the Mint's gold to come from American soil, yet hundreds of millions of dollars worth of foreign gold has reportedly entered the supply chain. Some of this gold originates from cartel-controlled mines in northwestern Colombia, where workers use toxic mercury that harms both laborers and the environment.

Perhaps most disturbing, Canadian authorities acknowledged that even if they violated their own guidelines, they would face "no legal consequences" because manipulating the "North American" classification isn't technically a crime.
