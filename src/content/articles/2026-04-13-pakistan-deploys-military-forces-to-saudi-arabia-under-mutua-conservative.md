Pakistan has deployed military personnel and fighter jets to Saudi Arabia in a show of solidarity following recent Iranian strikes on the kingdom's energy facilities. The deployment to King Abdulaziz Air Base represents a strengthening of defense ties between the two nations under a mutual defense agreement signed last September.

According to the Saudi Defense Ministry, the Pakistani forces arrived Saturday with a mission to enhance military coordination and support regional security and stability. The deployment comes in direct response to Iranian missile and drone attacks that targeted Saudi energy infrastructure and killed a Saudi national. Pakistan strongly condemned these strikes as "a dangerous escalation" that threatens peace in the region.

Under the defense pact between Riyadh and Islamabad, any attack on either nation is considered an attack on both—a mutual defense commitment that echoes traditional military alliances. The partnership demonstrates how nations can work together to counter Iranian aggression and protect critical infrastructure vital to global energy markets.

The deepening military cooperation coincides with significant economic ties between the nations.
