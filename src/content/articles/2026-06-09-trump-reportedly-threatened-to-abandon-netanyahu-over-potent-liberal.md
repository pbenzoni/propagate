President Donald Trump reportedly warned Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu that the United States might withdraw its support if Israel reignites a full-scale war with Iran, according to a report by Axios on Monday. The threat came during what sources described as tense phone conversations between the two leaders.

The warnings follow Iran's announcement last week that it was suspending diplomatic talks with the U.S. after repeated Israeli strikes in Lebanon, where nearly 3,700 people have been killed since early March. Iran has made clear that any peace agreement would require the cessation of hostilities on "all fronts," including in Lebanon, where violence has escalated in response to attacks involving Hezbollah.

The president has previously acknowledged calling Netanyahu "f**king crazy" during an earlier conversation. Trump also told BBC's North America editor that he urged the Israeli leader to "use common sense," noting that he was "very close to signing a very powerful deal" with Iran.

Multiple reports indicate that Trump attempted to dissuade Netanyahu from retaliating after Iran launched 11 missiles, all of which were intercepted.
