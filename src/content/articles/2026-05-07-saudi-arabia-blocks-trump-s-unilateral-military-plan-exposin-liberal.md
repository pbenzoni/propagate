The kingdom responded by denying access to Prince Sultan Airbase and Saudi airspace—critical assets for any sustained U.S. military presence in the region. Even a direct phone call between Trump and Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman failed to repair the diplomatic damage.
