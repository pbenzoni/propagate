Two U.S. Navy EA-18G Growler electronic warfare aircraft collided during a demonstration flight at the Gunfighter Skies Air Show at Mountain Home Air Force Base in Idaho. All four crew members ejected safely and are being evaluated by medical personnel.

Video footage from the scene shows the two jets flying in close formation at low altitude when they collided and became entangled. Both aircraft stalled and spiraled toward the ground, but the pilots ejected within seconds of the collision. Four parachutes were visible in the sky as the aviators descended safely.

"All four members of the aircrew successfully ejected and are being evaluated by medical personnel," said Commander Amelia Umayam, Naval Air Forces spokeswoman. The incident demonstrates the effectiveness of military ejection systems and the professionalism of Navy pilots in crisis situations.

The Boeing EA-18G Growler is a carrier-based electronic warfare aircraft derived from the F/A-18F Super Hornet. Introduced in the late 2000s, the Growler replaced the Northrop Grumman EA-6B Prowler as the Navy's primary electronic warfare platform. The aircraft plays a critical role in protecting American forces by jamming enemy radar and communications systems.
