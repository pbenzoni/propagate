The fund, established through a Justice Department settlement resolving Trump's lawsuit over leaked tax records, aims to compensate individuals allegedly subjected to politically motivated investigations and prosecutions. The $1.776 billion figure—a symbolic nod to America's founding—would be overseen by a five-member commission with authority to review claims and authorize payments to those who can demonstrate they were illegally targeted by the federal government.
