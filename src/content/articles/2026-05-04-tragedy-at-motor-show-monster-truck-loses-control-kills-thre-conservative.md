A motor show in Colombia turned deadly Sunday when a monster truck driver lost control of her vehicle during a stunt performance, plowing through safety barriers and into a crowd of spectators. At least three people were killed and 38 others injured in the tragic accident in Popayan, southwest of Bogota.

Footage circulating online shows the massive vehicle navigating an obstacle course during an exhibition featuring modified cars and motorcycles. According to local authorities, the driver was unable to brake following a stunt, and the truck suddenly veered toward the crowd after knocking down metal barriers meant to separate the public from the track.

"We are deeply saddened by this accident… which, according to preliminary figures, has caused more than 38 injuries and three deaths," said Popayan mayor Juan Carlos Munoz. Among the victims was a ten-year-old girl, according to a city official who spoke with Colombian newspaper El Espectador. Several other minors were among the injured and receiving treatment at local medical facilities.

The incident raises serious questions about safety protocols at public motor shows and whether adequate precautions were in place to protect spectators, particularly families with children. Fire department officials have secured the area while utility crews work to restore electricity damaged in the accident.
