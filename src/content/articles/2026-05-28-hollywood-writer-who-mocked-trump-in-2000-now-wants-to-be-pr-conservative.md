Dan Greaney, a longtime writer for The Simpsons who penned a 2000 episode portraying Donald Trump's presidency as a cautionary tale, has announced his own bid for the White House in 2028.

Greaney wrote the "Bart to the Future" episode that aired in March 2000, which depicted an adult Lisa Simpson as president dealing with a "budget crunch from President Trump." The episode has been frequently cited by critics as an example of Hollywood's longstanding antipathy toward the 45th president.

In a video posted to social media Tuesday, Greaney appeared dressed as a prophet before revealing a suit underneath, announcing he would run as what he calls a "progressive Republican." He criticized President Trump, Vice President J.D. Vance, and members of both parties, claiming they have abandoned America's founding principles.

According to his campaign website, the Hollywood writer—who attended law school and passed the bar—is running on a platform he calls "America for All." His stated priorities include campaign finance restrictions, expanded government healthcare programs, and increased housing regulations. The announcement marks yet another entertainment industry figure attempting to leverage media credentials into political office, though Greaney's self-described "progressive" label raises questions about his actual commitment to Republican principles.
