The Pentagon released its third batch of declassified UFO files on Friday, adding 72 reports to an ongoing transparency initiative ordered by President Donald Trump earlier this year. The release includes witness accounts from military personnel, photographs, and videos of unidentified anomalous phenomena—the term defense officials prefer over UFOs.

Among the newly disclosed materials are reports of unusual sightings by service members, including descriptions of a "potato-shaped" object, recurring observations of glowing orbs, and a CIA account of a rotating disc-like object reportedly seen above an international airport in Zimbabwe in 2008. The documents represent part of what officials are calling a "historic transparency effort" to declassify government records related to unexplained aerial phenomena.

The disclosure initiative follows years of congressional oversight and testimony from military whistleblowers who alleged the government was concealing information about mysterious objects observed near sensitive defense installations. In February, President Trump directed the Department of Defense to release "any and all information" related to these phenomena, marking a significant shift toward government accountability on the issue.

The release came after former President Barack Obama made public comments about the possibility of extraterrestrial life, later clarifying on social media that he saw no evidence of alien contact during his presidency. Trump criticized Obama for potentially disclosing classified information, stating he "made a big mistake."

Despite the substantial volume of released material—now totaling over 300 documents since May—Pentagon officials maintain the files contain no confirmed evidence of extraterrestrial life, alien technology, or government conspiracy. Most reports have been attributed to conventional explanations including camera distortions, aircraft signatures, military exercises, weather balloons, and classified test flights, though some cases remain unresolved due to insufficient data.
