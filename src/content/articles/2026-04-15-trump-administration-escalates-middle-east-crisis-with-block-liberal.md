The aggressive military action comes after diplomatic negotiations brokered by Pakistan failed to produce a resolution.

Admiral Brad Cooper, CENTCOM Commander, claimed Tuesday that U.S. forces have "completely halted economic trade going into and out of Iran by sea" in less than 36 hours. However, reporting from The Wall Street Journal indicates that more than 20 commercial vessels have passed through the strait in the past 24 hours, raising questions about the scope and effectiveness of the blockade.

Iran has demanded recognition of its sovereignty over the strait and the right to impose tolls on vessels using the waterway. While a temporary two-week ceasefire agreed upon on April 8 raised hopes for de-escalation, both sides have since accused each other of proposing unacceptable terms.
