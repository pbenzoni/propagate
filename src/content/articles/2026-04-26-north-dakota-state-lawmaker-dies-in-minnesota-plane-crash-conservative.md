A tragic plane crash near Minneapolis claimed the lives of North Dakota state Representative Liz Conmy, 67, and an unidentified pilot on Saturday, according to local authorities and the Associated Press.

The Federal Aviation Administration confirmed that a single-engine Beechcraft BE33 crashed shortly after departing from Crystal Airport with two people aboard. Security camera footage circulating online appears to show the aircraft crashing into a wooded area before erupting into flames.

North Dakota Governor Kelly Armstrong expressed condolences in a statement, noting that "Liz served her state and community with care and compassion." The governor added that North Dakota is "saddened by this tragic loss and offer our deepest condolences to her family, friends and fellow legislators."

Conmy's party, the North Dakota Democratic–Nonpartisan League Party, called her death "a profound loss to the state" in a social media post. The US National Transportation Safety Board will lead the investigation into what caused the crash.
