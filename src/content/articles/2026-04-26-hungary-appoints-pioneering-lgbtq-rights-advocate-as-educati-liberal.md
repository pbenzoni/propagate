Hungary's incoming Prime Minister Peter Magyar has appointed Judit Lannert as Minister of Education and Child Care Affairs, marking a significant departure from the policies of former leader Viktor Orbán. Lannert, a longtime critic of the previous government, has been recognized for her advocacy on LGBTQ+ issues and her support for Ukraine.

The appointment comes at a pivotal moment for Hungarian education policy. Under Orbán's leadership, Hungary enacted sweeping restrictions on LGBTQ+-related content accessible to minors, including a controversial 2021 child protection law that limited how gender and sexuality topics could be discussed in schools and media. Critics argued these measures stigmatized LGBTQ+ individuals and families.

The court found that the measures "stigmatize and marginalize LGBTQ individuals" while violating human dignity and freedom of expression, ordering Hungary to roll back the restrictions.

Lannert's selection suggests a potential realignment of Hungarian education policy with broader European human rights standards. Her previous criticism of Orbán-era policies and public support for inclusive values—including displaying LGBTQ+-themed colors on social media during debates over constitutional amendments—indicate she may pursue a different approach to education and child welfare that respects diverse families and identities.
