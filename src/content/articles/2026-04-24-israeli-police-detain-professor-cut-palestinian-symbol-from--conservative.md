A professor at Hebrew University of Jerusalem reported being detained by Israeli police after a cafe patron complained about his religious head covering, which displayed both Israeli and Palestinian flags—a design he says he had worn without incident for two decades.

Alex Sinclair recounted on social media that police arrived at the Modi'in cafe within minutes of being called and immediately informed him his kippah violated the law. When he refused to surrender the religious garment, which he described as personally meaningful, officers detained him. Though he was quickly released, police attempted to retain the head covering.

According to Sinclair's account, when he demanded its return, an officer cut out the Palestinian flag portion before giving it back—despite the symbol not being illegal under Israeli law. The incident raises questions about the balance between expanded public order enforcement powers and religious freedom protections.

Israeli police confirmed detaining a man over Palestinian flag imagery but declined further comment. Since the October 2023 Hamas attack that triggered the current Gaza conflict, Israeli authorities have significantly broadened law enforcement powers related to public order, with multiple reports of officers removing Palestinian flags from public spaces. The professor has filed a formal complaint seeking compensation and assurance he can wear his religious head covering without harassment.
