Austria deployed Eurofighter Typhoon jets to intercept two U.S. Air Force reconnaissance aircraft that entered its airspace without authorization over the weekend, according to the country's Defense Ministry.

Defense Ministry spokesman Michael Bauer confirmed that a pair of PC-12 turboprop surveillance planes flew over the Totes Gebirge mountains in Upper Austria on Sunday and Monday. The aircraft returned to German airspace after the interception. Bauer stated that Austrian officials would address the airspace violation "through diplomatic channels."

The incident has sparked debate within Austria about respect for the nation's longstanding neutrality. When critics on social media suggested Austria's response was insufficient, Bauer defended the measured approach, questioning whether critics expected military force against allied aircraft. "If you're driving too fast on the highway, do you expect the police to shoot you, or just give you a fine?" he wrote.

The unauthorized flights come weeks after Austria denied U.S. requests for overflight rights during military operations against Iran, with Austrian Vice Chancellor Andrea Babler emphasizing the importance of maintaining the country's neutral status amid what he characterized as unpredictable policy shifts.
