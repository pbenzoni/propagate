The incident occurred as the vessel, identified as the Touska, attempted to navigate through the Strait of Hormuz while returning from China. The seizure followed President Trump's announcement last week of a naval blockade of Iranian ports—a move that came after Pakistani-mediated diplomatic efforts failed to produce results. Iranian officials characterized the action as "armed maritime piracy" and warned of potential retaliation.

The legal framework of naval blockades under international law requires careful distinction between military and civilian targets, raising concerns among human rights observers about the impact on ordinary Iranians.

Iranian forces reportedly launched drones toward US naval vessels in response, though the Pentagon has not confirmed any attacks.
