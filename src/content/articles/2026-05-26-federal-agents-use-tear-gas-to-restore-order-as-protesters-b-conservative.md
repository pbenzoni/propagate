Federal immigration agents deployed tear gas and pepper-ball projectiles to disperse protesters blocking access to a Newark, New Jersey detention facility on Monday, after days of escalating unrest outside the facility. The Department of Homeland Security said officers issued multiple lawful commands for protesters to clear the area after they obstructed law enforcement from exiting the building.

Democratic Senator Andy Kim was present at the scene and reported being caught in pepper spray during the incident. Video footage shows Kim standing between protesters and Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents during an arrest. DHS emphasized in a statement that no one was directly struck by pepper ball projectiles and that agents acted only after protesters refused to comply with lawful orders.

Protesters claimed inmates were protesting conditions including food quality and medical care. The incident comes amid President Trump's enforcement campaign against illegal immigration, which has resulted in thousands of arrests nationwide.
