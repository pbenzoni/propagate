President Donald Trump declared that Iran has been "completely defeated" militarily, warning the regime it will face consequences for dragging out negotiations with Washington. The statement came as Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps launched missile and drone strikes against American military facilities across the Middle East.

US Central Command reported that the Iranian strikes came in retaliation for American operations inside Iran, which were conducted after an Apache helicopter was lost near the Strait of Hormuz.

Jordan and Kuwait reported intercepting incoming projectiles. This exchange represents the most serious escalation since a ceasefire was announced in early April, with peace efforts now stalled as both sides accuse each other of violations.

Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesman said the US strikes had undermined negotiations and forced Tehran to "reassess" its participation in talks. Tehran insists its nuclear program remains peaceful.
