The core tension revolves around competing visions for Ukraine's future. While some frameworks envision Ukraine pursuing European integration while abandoning claims to currently occupied territories, Ukrainian leaders and their supporters emphasize that any sustainable peace must respect Ukraine's sovereignty and the right of displaced populations to return home. Human rights advocates note that approximately 20% of Ukraine's pre-war territory remains under occupation, affecting millions of civilians.

International legal experts emphasize that peace agreements imposed without genuine consent risk creating precedents that undermine the rules-based international order.

The path forward remains uncertain. While ending the violence is urgent, sustainable peace requires addressing root causes: respecting sovereignty, ensuring accountability for violations of international law, and protecting the rights of affected populations.
