The controversy stems from Polanski's decision to share, without comment, a video showing police officers forcefully restraining a suspect during a stabbing incident in London's Golders Green neighborhood. The suspect, a Muslim man with a history of mental illness, had injured a Muslim acquaintance and attacked two Jewish men. Metropolitan Police Commissioner Mark Rowley accused Polanski of spreading "inaccurate and misinformed commentary" that undermines confidence in law enforcement. Prime Minister Keir Starmer called the implied criticism "disgraceful."

Critics argue the depiction echoes features historically associated with anti-Jewish stereotypes. The Green Party called the decision to publish "astonishing," while Polanski himself described the cartoon as "vile" and "anti-Semitic."
