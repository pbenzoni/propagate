Ed Gallrein, a military veteran and former Navy SEAL endorsed by President Donald Trump, has defeated 12-year incumbent Representative Thomas Massie in Kentucky's Republican primary election. Gallrein secured victory with 54.9% of the vote compared to Massie's 45.1% and will face Democrat Melissa Strange in November's general election.

The primary became the most expensive in American history, with nearly $33 million spent on advertising as outside groups poured resources into the race. According to Federal Election Commission filings, pro-Israel organizations including the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) and the Republican Jewish Coalition contributed $15.5 million supporting Gallrein's campaign.

President Trump invested significant political capital in the race, personally campaigning for Gallrein in Kentucky in March and sending Secretary of War Pete Hegseth to a rally in Hebron on the eve of the election. Trump had announced his opposition to Massie last year after the congressman pressured him to release unredacted Epstein files and refused to support the administration's 'One Big Beautiful Bill' spending package.

Massie, who had easily won five previous primary challenges since first taking office in 2012, characterized the election as "a referendum on whether Israel gets to buy seats in Congress." In his concession speech, Massie took a parting shot at his opponent, joking that it "took a while to find Ed Gallrein in Tel Aviv" when calling to concede.

The victory further solidifies Trump's influence over Republican primary contests. In another Kentucky race Tuesday, Trump-endorsed Ralph Alvarado won his primary to replace outgoing Representative Andy Barr. Last week, Louisiana Senator Bill Cassidy lost to Trump-backed challenger Julia Letlow, while the president has also endorsed former Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton in next week's Senate primary against John Cornyn.
