Two members of London's Jewish community were stabbed Wednesday morning in what authorities are investigating as a terrorist hate crime in the Golders Green neighborhood, an area that has experienced a troubling pattern of antisemitic violence in recent months.

Prime Minister Keir Starmer condemned the attack as "utterly appalling," while Metropolitan Police Commissioner Mark Rowley confirmed the incident is "being treated as a terrorist attack." The two victims—men in their 30s and 70s—were hospitalized and are reportedly in stable condition. Counter Terrorism Policing specialists are now leading the investigation.

Members of Shomrim, a Jewish neighborhood watch organization, initially restrained the attacker before police arrival. Israeli President Isaac Herzog expressed alarm at what he characterized as escalating danger for Jewish people in London, calling on the UK government to take "urgent and immediate action."
