Thousands of demonstrators took to the streets of Geneva on Sunday to protest the Group of Seven summit scheduled to take place this week in nearby Evian-les-Bains, France. The march, which organizers estimated drew around 20,000 participants, brought together approximately 60 organizations representing labor unions, feminist groups, and advocates for Palestinian and Kurdish statehood.

The largely peaceful demonstration along the shores of Lake Geneva turned confrontational when riot police deployed water cannons and tear gas against protesters. Videos from the scene showed vandalism including smashed bank windows and a Tesla vehicle that caught fire during the unrest, though it remains unclear whether the car was deliberately targeted.

Demonstrators chanted slogans opposing fascism, imperialism, and capitalism, with one banner depicting President Donald Trump as a dinosaur consuming the planet. The protest reflects growing criticism of the G7 format, which some argue has become outdated as it fails to include major emerging economies like China, making the more inclusive G20 a more relevant forum for addressing global challenges.

Swiss authorities closed 25 of 35 border crossings with France citing security concerns ahead of the summit. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is expected to attend the G7 meeting to seek additional international support, with reports suggesting Ukraine hopes to secure up to $20 billion in funding from NATO countries.
