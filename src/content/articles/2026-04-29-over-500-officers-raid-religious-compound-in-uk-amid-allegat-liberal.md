More than 500 British police officers executed search warrants at multiple locations in Cheshire this week, including the headquarters of a small religious sect, following allegations of serious sexual offenses, forced marriage, and modern slavery. Nine individuals of various nationalities were taken into custody.

According to Cheshire Police, the investigation was launched in March 2024 after authorities received reports of crimes alleged to have occurred in 2023. The allegations involve a single victim, a woman who was a member of the group at the time. The arrested individuals include six men and three women holding American, Mexican, Italian, Spanish, Egyptian, and Swedish citizenship.

The organization in question, known as the Ahmadi Religion of Peace and Light, is a secretive movement with an estimated 7,000 followers worldwide. The group relocated to the UK from Sweden in 2021 after Swedish immigration authorities issued deportation orders against 69 of its members. British Home Office officials are reportedly investigating the group's use of skilled worker visas. The organization has denied all allegations of wrongdoing.

This case is part of a broader pattern of accountability efforts addressing sexual violence within positions of religious authority. Earlier this year, a former imam in east London was convicted on 21 counts including rape of children and sexual assault, while in 2023 another religious leader in Birmingham was imprisoned for sexually assaulting a vulnerable woman seeking spiritual guidance. The UK government has also authorized a national inquiry into systemic child sexual exploitation, with thousands of cases under review across the country.
