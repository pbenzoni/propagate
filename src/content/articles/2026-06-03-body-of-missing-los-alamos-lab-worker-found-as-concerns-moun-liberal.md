The remains of Melissa Casias, an administrative assistant at Los Alamos National Laboratory, have been found in Carson National Forest, New Mexico State Police confirmed. A hiker discovered the remains on May 28, along with a handgun at the scene. Casias had been missing since late June 2025, after failing to arrive at work and leaving behind her purse, identification, and cell phones.

Casias's death is part of a deeply concerning pattern that has prompted congressional oversight. At least ten individuals connected to classified advanced research facilities have died or disappeared under unusual circumstances in recent years, leading the House Oversight Committee to request briefings from multiple federal agencies including the Department of Energy, FBI, and NASA.

Among the cases: another Los Alamos employee, Anthony Chavez, 78, remains missing since May 2025. Steven Garcia, a contractor at a facility producing components for nuclear weapons, vanished from his Albuquerque home in August 2025. NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory workers have also been affected, with materials official Monica Reza reported missing in 2023 and two colleagues dying since then with no public cause of death disclosed.

The series of incidents has raised alarms about potential threats to scientific personnel working on sensitive national security projects. While authorities have not yet established any confirmed connection among the cases, the concentration of disappearances and deaths among this specialized workforce has triggered calls for greater transparency and protection measures for researchers working on classified programs.
