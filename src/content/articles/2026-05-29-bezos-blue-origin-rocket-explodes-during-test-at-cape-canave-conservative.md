Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket exploded in a massive fireball during a preflight static fire test at Cape Canaveral, Florida on Thursday. The dramatic incident was captured on live stream footage, showing the heavy-lift rocket engulfed in flames on the launchpad.

Jeff Bezos' aerospace company confirmed that "an anomaly" had occurred during the test but reported that all personnel were safely accounted for. The explosion represents a significant setback for the company's commercial space ambitions and raises questions about the reliability of their launch systems.

The rocket was being prepared for its fourth launch mission, tentatively scheduled for next month. According to reports, the launch was intended to deploy 48 satellites for Amazon's Leo internet service. Fortunately, the satellites were not aboard the rocket at the time of the explosion.

The New Glenn rocket features a reusable first-stage booster, part of Blue Origin's effort to compete in the increasingly crowded commercial space industry. The cause of the explosion remains under investigation.
