Senator Lindsey Graham has proposed a bold strategy to help Iranian citizens challenge their authoritarian government from within, calling on President Trump to provide weapons that would enable ordinary Iranians to stand up to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps.

Speaking on Fox News with Sean Hannity Monday evening, the South Carolina Republican framed his proposal as a "Second Amendment solution" for Iran—a way to empower the Iranian people to defend themselves without deploying American ground troops. "If I were President Trump and I were Israel, I would load the Iranian people up with weapons so they can go to the streets armed and turn the tide of battle inside Iran," Graham stated.

The senator acknowledged that a similar covert effort had been attempted previously. President Trump confirmed last month that the US had sent firearms to Iranian protesters during nationwide unrest in late 2025, though those weapons were reportedly stolen and the regime-change effort ultimately failed. Despite these setbacks—including the assassination of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei in February—Graham insisted the approach should be tried again.

"I love the idea of empowering the Iranian people with weapons," Graham said, arguing that armed citizens could make life difficult for the IRGC. "It's one thing to be bombed by America. It's another thing to have your neighbor shoot back at you," he added. Graham has long advocated for aggressive measures against Iran's government, previously calling for strikes on the country's military infrastructure.

Critics of such interventions argue that arming foreign populations risks unintended consequences and civilian harm. A geostrategic analyst who recently visited Iran told RT that strikes on civilian infrastructure including medical facilities and schools suggest broader destabilization goals beyond regime change.
