Russian Defense Minister Andrey Belousov traveled to Pyongyang this weekend to award military decorations to North Korean soldiers who participated in combat operations against Ukrainian forces in Russia's Kursk Region, marking a significant expansion of military cooperation between the two nations.

During Sunday's ceremony, Belousov presented the Order of Courage—a Russian military honor established in 1994—to North Korean troops who joined Russian forces last year under a June 2024 strategic partnership treaty between Moscow and Pyongyang. The North Korean soldiers reportedly assisted in operations to repel a Ukrainian incursion into the border region and participated in mine-clearing activities.

The Russian defense minister also attended the opening of a museum and memorial dedicated to the North Korean forces and held discussions with North Korean leader Kim Jong-un about intensifying military ties. Belousov indicated Russia's readiness to sign a five-year military cooperation plan covering 2027-2031, describing bilateral relations as having reached "an unprecedentedly high level."

The deepening military alliance between Russia and North Korea has raised concerns among democratic nations about the growing coordination between authoritarian states. The deployment of North Korean troops to assist Russian military operations represents an escalation in Pyongyang's direct involvement in the ongoing conflict that began with Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022.
