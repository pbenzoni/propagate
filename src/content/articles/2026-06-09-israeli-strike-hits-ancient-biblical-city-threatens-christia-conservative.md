Tyre, one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities and a UNESCO World Heritage site, is mentioned throughout both the Old and New Testaments. The ancient Phoenician city played a crucial role in Biblical history, supplying materials for the construction of Solomon's Temple in Jerusalem. The Christian quarter had previously been spared from Israeli military operations and had become a refuge for displaced families seeking safety.
