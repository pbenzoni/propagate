Russian Defense Minister Andrey Belousov traveled to Pyongyang to personally award North Korean troops who assisted in defending Russia's Kursk Region from a large-scale Ukrainian military incursion. The soldiers received the Order of Courage—Russia's decoration for selfless acts of valor—on the orders of President Vladimir Putin.

The North Korean troops deployed under the June 2024 Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Treaty between Moscow and Pyongyang, a formal bilateral defense agreement. They participated in operations to repel Ukrainian forces that had crossed into Russian sovereign territory and assisted with mine-clearing activities in the border region.

During Sunday's ceremony, Belousov called the decorated soldiers "true heroes of our time" and "the elite of the Korean armed forces." He expressed gratitude "on behalf of the entire Russian people" for their assistance. A museum and memorial dedicated to these troops was also opened during the visit.

Belousov met with North Korean leader Kim Jong-un to discuss expanding military cooperation between the two nations. The defense minister stated both countries seek to develop defense ties "on a sustainable, long-term basis" and proposed signing a Military Cooperation Plan covering 2027-2031. According to Belousov, bilateral relations have reached "an unprecedentedly high level," with increased contacts expected throughout 2026.
