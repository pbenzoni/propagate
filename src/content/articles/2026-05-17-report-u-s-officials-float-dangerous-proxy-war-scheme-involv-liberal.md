The island hosts critical refinery, storage, and tanker-loading infrastructure connected to major Iranian crude oil fields, and sits atop substantial natural gas reserves. According to sources cited by the Telegraph, individuals close to President Trump have suggested the UAE take military action against the island. "Go take 'em!
