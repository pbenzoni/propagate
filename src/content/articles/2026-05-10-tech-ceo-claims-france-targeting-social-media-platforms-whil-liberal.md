Telegram co-founder Pavel Durov has accused French authorities of hypocrisy following the expansion of a criminal investigation into Elon Musk's social media platform X. The controversy centers on allegations that France is conducting the same type of data collection practices it accuses tech platforms of perpetrating.

French prosecutors announced plans to pursue additional charges against X related to alleged illegal deepfakes created by the platform's Grok AI system, as well as claims of unlawful data extraction. The investigation, which began in January, includes allegations that the platform's algorithms contain bias and accusations involving the possession and distribution of AI-generated sexualized imagery, including content involving minors. X has denied wrongdoing and characterized the legal action as politically motivated.

The U.S. Department of Justice reportedly declined a French request for cooperation in the investigation last month. Durov himself was arrested at a Paris airport in 2024 and indicted on multiple charges after French prosecutors alleged he was complicit in crimes committed using Telegram. He was subsequently permitted to leave France despite the ongoing investigation.

The case highlights ongoing tensions between European regulators and major tech platforms over content moderation, data privacy, and algorithmic accountability. Questions about how social media companies collect and use personal data, as well as concerns about AI-generated content and platform governance, remain at the forefront of digital policy debates across multiple jurisdictions.
