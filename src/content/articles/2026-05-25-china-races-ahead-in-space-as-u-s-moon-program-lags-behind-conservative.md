The crew, led by Commander Zhu Yangzhu and including former Air Force pilot Zhang Zhiyuan and Hong Kong police officer Lai Ka-ying, will spend six months conducting experiments and maintenance. Notably, one crew member will remain for a full year—China's longest orbital mission to date.
