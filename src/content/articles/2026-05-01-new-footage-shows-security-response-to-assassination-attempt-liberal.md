The Department of Justice has released high-quality security camera footage showing the moments when Cole Tomas Allen, 31, allegedly attempted to breach security at the Washington Hilton during the White House Correspondents' Dinner on Saturday. The event was attended by President Donald Trump, First Lady Melania Trump, White House officials, and members of the press.

The silent CCTV footage shows the suspect moving through a hallway before rushing past metal detector screening. At one point, he allegedly pointed a shotgun at security personnel, prompting return fire from a security guard's sidearm. Allen has been charged with attempting to assassinate the president.

Security personnel successfully subdued and restrained the suspect before he could reach the ballroom where the president was seated. According to the DOJ, a Secret Service officer wearing body armor was struck once in the chest during the incident.

According to law enforcement, Allen left behind written materials in which he criticized presidential policies and stated his intent to take action, though he did not identify Trump by name in those writings. The investigation into the incident continues.
