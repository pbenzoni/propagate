The crash occurred as the U.S. military continues enforcement operations related to Iranian oil shipments in the strategically critical waterway, which accounts for approximately 20% of global crude oil trade. The New York Times first reported the incident, though it remains unclear whether the helicopter was brought down by hostile fire or experienced a technical malfunction.
