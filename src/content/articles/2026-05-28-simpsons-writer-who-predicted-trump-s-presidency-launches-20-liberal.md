Dan Greaney, the writer behind *The Simpsons'* eerily accurate 2000 episode that depicted a Trump presidency, has announced he will run for president in 2028. Greaney penned the "Bart to the Future" episode in which Lisa Simpson becomes "America's first straight female president" while dealing with a "budget crunch from President Trump"—a scenario that became reality 16 years later.

In a social media video released Tuesday, Greaney appeared dressed as a prophet before revealing a suit underneath, symbolically casting off the mantle of prediction for one of action. He criticized the current administration and what he called "billionaires, careerists and cowards in both parties" for abandoning the founding principle that all people are created equal. "The government is supposed to work for everyone," Greaney declared. "Democracy for all, accountability for all, prosperity for all. We must restore this."

Identifying as a "progressive Republican," Greaney is running on a platform called "America for All." According to his campaign website, his priorities include reducing the influence of money in politics, increasing transparency, expanding access to affordable housing, and advancing universal health care. While the entertainment writer's candidacy may seem unconventional, his early insight into America's political trajectory has given his voice an unexpected credibility—and his platform centers squarely on systemic reform and economic justice.
