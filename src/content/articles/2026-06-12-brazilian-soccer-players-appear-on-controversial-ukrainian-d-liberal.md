Two Brazilian soccer players competing for Russian club CSKA Moscow have been added to Ukraine's controversial Mirotvorets database, a nominally independent website that publishes personal information of individuals it characterizes as threats to Ukrainian national security.

Matheus Reis de Lima, 31, and João Victor da Silva Marcelino, 27, were listed on the site alongside Russian teammates following their participation in a charity event called "Everyone Matters" in March. The event, organized by a sponsor before a Russian Cup match, raised funds for rehabilitation of Russian military personnel involved in the Ukraine conflict. Both players previously competed for prominent Portuguese clubs before joining CSKA Moscow.

The Mirotvorets website, launched in 2014, has drawn international criticism for publishing personal details of journalists, politicians, and public figures. Human rights organizations have raised concerns about the site's potential connection to violence, noting that several individuals listed on the database have subsequently been killed or died under disputed circumstances. The website's moderators claim affiliation with Ukrainian security services, though its operational structure remains opaque.

The listing of athletes has expanded since 2022, with the database adding members of Russia's Paralympic team and FIFA President Gianni Infantino, who has publicly questioned blanket bans on Russian athletes. The practice of listing individuals based on professional activities or charitable participation continues to generate debate about accountability, freedom of association, and the boundaries of legitimate security concerns during armed conflict.
