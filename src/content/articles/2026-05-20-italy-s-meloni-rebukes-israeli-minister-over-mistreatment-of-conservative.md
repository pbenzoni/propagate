Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni, a conservative leader who initially stood firmly with Israel following the October 2023 Hamas terror attacks, has summoned Israel's ambassador over disturbing footage showing detained activists being mistreated.

The controversy erupted after Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir posted video showing activists kneeling with their hands bound while he appeared to mock them. The detainees—including Italian citizens—were among 430 people from over 40 countries arrested when Israeli forces intercepted a Gaza-bound aid flotilla that departed from Turkey. In the footage, Ben-Gvir urged Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to keep the activists imprisoned "for a long, long time."

Meloni condemned the treatment as "unacceptable" and "inadmissible," stating it "violates human dignity." She demanded the immediate release of Italian citizens, formal clarifications from Israel's ambassador, and an apology for what she called "total contempt" toward explicit requests from the Italian government. While Meloni remains one of Israel's strongest EU supporters in its fight against Hamas terrorism, she has increasingly raised concerns about proportionality in Gaza operations and the humanitarian situation there.

Netanyahu later distanced himself from Ben-Gvir's conduct, saying it "is not consistent with the values and norms of the State of Israel." Foreign Minister Gideon Saar called the display "disgraceful," acknowledging it "caused harm" to Israel. Israeli officials defended the flotilla interception itself as necessary, describing the convoy as a "PR stunt at the service of Hamas."
