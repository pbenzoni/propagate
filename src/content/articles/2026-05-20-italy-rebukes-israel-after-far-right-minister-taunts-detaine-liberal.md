Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni has summoned Israel's ambassador to demand answers after video emerged showing Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir openly mocking humanitarian activists detained by Israeli forces. The activists, including Italian citizens, were part of an international flotilla attempting to deliver aid to Gaza when Israeli forces intercepted their vessels.

In a forceful statement, Meloni called the footage "unacceptable" and condemned the treatment of the detainees as a violation of human dignity. "It is inadmissible that these demonstrators, including many Italian citizens, are subjected to this treatment," she wrote. Italy is demanding the immediate release of its citizens, formal clarification from Israeli officials, and an apology for what Meloni described as "total contempt" toward Italian government requests.

The video posted by Ben-Gvir shows activists kneeling with their hands bound while the far-right minister appears to taunt them, urging Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to keep them imprisoned "for a long, long time." Netanyahu later sought to distance himself from the incident, stating Ben-Gvir's conduct was inconsistent with Israeli values. Around 430 activists from more than 40 countries were detained after their humanitarian convoy departed from Turkey.

The diplomatic crisis highlights growing international concern over Israel's conduct in Gaza. Meloni, once among Israel's strongest EU supporters following the October 2023 Hamas attacks, has increasingly criticized Israeli actions, describing the humanitarian situation as "unacceptable" and warning that Israel has exceeded "the principle of proportionality." Human rights groups have also raised alarms about rising settler violence in the occupied West Bank and what they characterize as Israeli authorities' failure to hold perpetrators accountable.
