US Central Command chief Admiral Brad Cooper offered strong praise for America's Middle Eastern allies during joint operations against Iranian aggression, singling out Israel as an exemplary partner. Speaking at the Pentagon alongside Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth, Cooper emphasized the effectiveness of the military coalition.

"There's been no better teammate than Israel," Cooper stated, also commending Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, and the UAE as "exceptional teammates." The admiral highlighted how coordination between the Israeli Air Force and American military branches achieved their objectives, calling the combined forces "the four best air forces in the world."

Cooper noted his recent meeting with Israel's Chief of the General Staff, Lieutenant General Eyal Zamir, to maintain close alignment between the two nations. The coordination comes as a two-week truce nears expiration, with reports suggesting possible additional peace negotiations.

Secretary Hegseth pushed back against suggestions that the administration had been pressured into military action, stating that "President Trump makes his own decisions about the interests of this country and defending our interests, and then works with allies and partners." He expressed gratitude for Israel's capabilities and commitment to the partnership. Critics, including some from both parties and former National Counterterrorism Center chief Joe Kent, have questioned aspects of the operations. Iranian officials have characterized the conflict as contrary to America First principles, though administration officials maintain the actions serve US national security interests.
