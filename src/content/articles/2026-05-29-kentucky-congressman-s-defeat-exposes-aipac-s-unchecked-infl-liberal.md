The recent primary defeat of seven-term Kentucky Congressman Thomas Massie has reignited concerns about the outsized influence of foreign policy lobbying groups on American democracy. Massie, who served continuously since 2012, lost to political newcomer Ed Gallrein in a race that drew unusual national attention after President Trump personally campaigned against the incumbent.

Massie broke with his party on multiple issues, including pandemic response policies and military escalation with Iran, demonstrating the kind of independent judgment many voters claim to want from their representatives.

Yet his independence came at a cost.
